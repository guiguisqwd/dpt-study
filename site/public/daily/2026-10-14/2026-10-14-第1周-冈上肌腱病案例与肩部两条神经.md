#### Ms Grant, 48: right shoulder pain while stocking shelves｜Grant 女士，48 岁：理货时右肩痛

- **S Subjective 主观**：Ms Grant, 48, right-handed, stacks shelves in a supermarket. Right shoulder pain for 3 weeks, starting gradually after a week of restocking high shelves; no fall or injury. The pain is over the outer upper arm ([deltoid](http://127.0.0.1:5178/?term=deltoid) region) and does not go past the elbow. It is worse when reaching to shoulder height or overhead and when brushing her hair, and settles within about 30 minutes of rest. Worst pain 6/10. It wakes her about twice a week when she rolls onto the right side; she gets back to sleep. No neck pain, no pins and needles or numbness, no feeling that the shoulder might slip out, no sudden loss of the ability to lift the arm. No fever, weight loss or history of cancer. Her goal: full shifts including the top shelves, and sleeping on her right side.<br>Grant 女士，48 岁，右利手，在超市理货。右肩痛 3 周，是在集中补了一周高层货架之后慢慢开始的，没有摔倒或受伤。疼痛在上臂外侧（三角肌区），不过肘。往肩部高度或头顶上方够东西、梳头时加重，休息约 30 分钟后缓解。最痛 6/10。每周大约有两次翻身压到右侧时痛醒，之后能再睡着。没有颈痛，没有发麻或刺痛，没有肩要脱出的感觉，没有突然抬不起手臂。没有发烧、体重下降或癌症史。她的目标：上满班（包括高层货架），能侧躺右边睡觉。
- **O Objective 客观**：Observation: no swelling, redness or deformity; muscle bulk in the supraspinous and infraspinous fossae is the same on both sides. Neck movements are full and do not change the arm pain. Palpation: tender below the [acromion](http://127.0.0.1:5178/?term=acromion) (subacromial); the acromioclavicular joint is not tender. Active abduction reaches full range but is painful between about 70° and 100° (painful arc). Passive range is full and pain-free on both sides, including external rotation with the arm by the side. Resisted abduction and resisted external rotation reproduce her pain, with strength equal to the left. No external rotation lag sign. [Deltoid](http://127.0.0.1:5178/?term=deltoid) strength and sensation over the lateral upper arm (the badge area) are normal.<br>望诊：无肿胀、发红或畸形；两侧冈上窝、冈下窝的肌肉体积一样。颈部活动全范围，不改变手臂疼痛。触诊：肩峰下方压痛；肩锁关节无压痛。主动外展能到全范围，但约 70° 到 100° 之间疼痛（疼痛弧）。两侧被动活动度都是全范围且不痛，包括手臂放在体侧时的外旋。抗阻外展和抗阻外旋诱发出她平时的疼痛，力量与左侧相同。外旋滞后征阴性。三角肌力量和上臂外侧（徽章区）感觉正常。
- **A Assessment 评估**：Working diagnosis: subacromial pain syndrome, with the [supraspinatus](http://127.0.0.1:5178/?term=supraspinatus) tendon the likely source ([supraspinatus](http://127.0.0.1:5178/?term=supraspinatus) tendinopathy). Naming the tendon is a hypothesis: no single clinical test can make a pathognomonic diagnosis (Hegedus 2012), and the Dutch guideline counts [supraspinatus](http://127.0.0.1:5178/?term=supraspinatus) tendinopathy as one of the labels under subacromial pain syndrome (Diercks 2014). No red flags. Nothing points to a full-thickness tear, frozen shoulder, osteoarthritis, pain from the neck, or a suprascapular or axillary nerve lesion (see the table below). Low irritability: the pain settles within about 30 minutes, so she does not fit the irritable picture Lewis describes, of easily aggravated pain that lasts once provoked, with night pain (our judgement).<br>工作诊断：肩峰下疼痛综合征，最可能的来源是冈上肌腱（冈上肌腱病）。把来源定为这条肌腱只是假设：没有哪一项临床检查能单独确诊（Hegedus 2012）；荷兰指南把冈上肌腱病算作肩峰下疼痛综合征下面的一种名称（Diercks 2014）。无红旗征。没有表现指向全层撕裂、冻结肩、骨关节炎、颈源性疼痛，或肩胛上神经、腋神经损伤（见下表）。激惹性低：疼痛约 30 分钟内缓解，不符合 Lewis 描述的高激惹表现（容易诱发、诱发后持续，并有夜间痛）；这是我们的判断。
- **P Plan 计划**：1. Explain: a clinical diagnosis; no imaging is needed to start; most symptoms respond within three months (BESS 2025). 2. Activity: keep moving within comfort and avoid tasks that are too long, heavy or frequent (Dutch guideline 2026); cut back on the top shelves for now and agree a return-to-work plan with her and her employer early (Lafrance 2022). 3. Exercise: rotator cuff exercises with an eccentric emphasis and scapular stabiliser exercises, at home once or twice a day; pain while exercising up to 5/10 and back to its usual level before the next session, otherwise lower the load (Holmgren 2012). 4. Review every 2 weeks; change one thing at a time and watch the next 24 hours (Lewis 2016). 5. When the plan changes: figure 01.<br>1. 解释：这是临床诊断；开始治疗不需要影像；大多数人的症状三个月内会好转（BESS 2025）。2. 活动：在舒服的范围内继续活动，避免时间太长、太重或太频繁的动作（荷兰指南 2026）；暂时少做高层货架，尽早和她及雇主一起定返岗计划（Lafrance 2022）。3. 运动：以离心为重点的肩袖训练加肩胛稳定肌训练，在家每天 1–2 次；练习时疼痛不超过 5/10，下次练习前回落到平时水平，否则减负荷（Holmgren 2012）。4. 每 2 周复查；一次只改一项，观察之后 24 小时的反应（Lewis 2016）。5. 什么时候改方案：见图 01。

The sources do not agree on how much pain is acceptable during exercise. The Dutch guideline says to stay within the pain threshold (Diercks 2014). The Holmgren trial allowed up to 5/10 if it settled before the next session. Lewis suggests that, in non-irritable presentations whose progress has plateaued, pain of about 2 to 5/10 may be tried if it settles soon after and does not increase pain at night or over 24 hours. The BESS review could not tell whether exercising below or above the pain threshold makes a clinically important difference. This case uses the Holmgren rule because that trial gives a complete protocol.

练习时能接受多少疼痛，来源之间不一致。荷兰指南说要在疼痛阈值以内（Diercks 2014）。Holmgren 试验允许到 5/10，前提是下次练习前能回落。Lewis 认为，低激惹且进展停滞的病人可以尝试约 2–5/10 的疼痛，但练后要很快缓解，并且不加重夜间痛和 24 小时内的疼痛。BESS 的证据回顾无法判断在疼痛阈值以下还是以上练习有没有临床上重要的差别。本例采用 Holmgren 的规定，因为这项试验给出了完整的方案。

No source opened for this pack gives numeric criteria for returning to overhead work. The case therefore uses criteria agreed with the patient: full shifts including the top shelves, with pain at work no higher than during exercise and no worse the next morning. These are a teaching choice, not a published standard. What the sources do say is to build a return-to-work plan early, with the worker and other stakeholders (Lafrance 2022), and to build up exercise within sport and work gradually, to raise the load the cuff can bear (Dutch guideline 2026).

本包打开过的来源都没有给出回到过头工作的量化标准。所以本例用和病人商定的标准：上满班（包括高层货架），工作时的疼痛不超过练习时，第二天早上不加重。这是教学上的选择，不是公开发表的标准。来源给出的是做法：尽早和劳动者及相关各方一起定返岗计划（Lafrance 2022）；在运动和工作中逐步加量，提高肩袖能承受的负荷（荷兰指南 2026）。

### Speaking practice 2: explaining to the patient｜口语练习 2：给病人解释

- **Physio · 治疗师**：From what you've told me and what I've found, this looks like an irritated tendon at the top of your shoulder, the [supraspinatus](http://127.0.0.1:5178/?term=supraspinatus) tendon.<br>根据你说的和我检查到的，这看起来是肩膀上方的一条肌腱受了刺激，叫冈上肌腱。
- **Physio · 治疗师**：It runs under the bony tip of your shoulder, the [acromion](http://127.0.0.1:5178/?term=acromion), and attaches to the top of your arm bone. It is often sore in the middle of lifting the arm to the side, as yours is.<br>这条肌腱从肩膀外侧那块骨头（肩峰）下面经过，止于上臂骨的上端。手臂向侧面抬到中段时常会痛，你就是这样。
- **Patient · 病人**：Do I need a scan?<br>我需要拍片子或做扫描吗？
- **Physio · 治疗师**：Not right now. This is diagnosed from your story and the examination, and a scan isn't needed to start treatment. If things change, we'll look at it again.<br>现在不需要。这个靠病史和查体诊断，开始治疗不需要影像。情况有变化，我们再考虑。

- **Physio · 治疗师**：Can you tell me in your own words what we're going to do?<br>你能用自己的话说说我们接下来要做什么吗？

The last line checks understanding: the patient repeats the plan in their own words, and you correct anything that is missing.

最后一句是确认病人听懂了：让病人用自己的话复述计划，漏了什么再补上。

</details>

Back to today's case: equal muscle bulk in both fossae and strong external rotation gave no sign of a suprascapular nerve lesion; normal [deltoid](http://127.0.0.1:5178/?term=deltoid) strength and badge-area sensation gave no sign of an axillary nerve lesion. In this week's chapter, resisted external rotation and the external rotation lag sign are listed for [Infraspinatus](http://127.0.0.1:5178/?term=infraspinatus) (suprascapular nerve), and Hornblower sign for [Teres minor](http://127.0.0.1:5178/?term=teres-minor) (axillary nerve).

回到今天的案例：两侧冈上窝、冈下窝肌肉对称，外旋有力，说明没有肩胛上神经损伤的表现；三角肌力量和徽章区感觉正常，说明没有腋神经损伤的表现。本周章节里，冈下肌（肩胛上神经）对应抗阻外旋和外旋滞后征，小圆肌（腋神经）对应 Hornblower 征。

> **Take-home 本章要点：** Two nerves from C5–C6, two routes: the suprascapular nerve leaves the superior trunk early and runs through two notches to two fossae; the axillary nerve leaves the posterior cord, goes through the quadrangular space and around the surgical neck to [Deltoid](http://127.0.0.1:5178/?term=deltoid) and [Teres minor](http://127.0.0.1:5178/?term=teres-minor).
