### Ligaments｜韧带

| Ligament 韧带 | From → to 起 → 止 | What it does or limits 作用或限制 | Source 来源 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Superior glenohumeral (SGHL)<br>盂肱上韧带 | Supraglenoid tubercle → upper lesser tubercle. | Middle glenohumeral (MGHL)<br>盂肱中韧带 | Anterior glenoid, just below the SGHL → lesser tubercle.<br>关节盂前缘、盂肱上韧带下方 → 小结节。 | Limits external rotation, especially with the arm abducted. Absent in about 30% of shoulders.<br>限制外旋，尤其在外展位。约 30% 的肩关节没有这条韧带。 | Kenhub; Radiopaedia<br>Kenhub；Radiopaedia |
| Inferior glenohumeral (IGHL)<br>盂肱下韧带 | A sling from the inferior two-thirds of the labrum → inferior anatomical neck; an anterior band and a posterior band, with the axillary pouch between them.<br>像吊床一样，从盂唇下 2/3 → 解剖颈下部；分前束和后束，两束之间是腋囊。 | The strongest of the three and the main stabiliser of the abducted shoulder. | Coracohumeral (CHL)<br>喙肱韧带 | Lateral base of the coracoid → greater and lesser tubercles (TeachMeAnatomy names only the greater tubercle). ### Where the sources differ｜来源不一致的地方

- Coracohumeral ligament insertion: the greater tubercle (TeachMeAnatomy) or both tubercles (Kenhub, StatPearls, Radiopaedia).<br>喙肱韧带的止点：大结节（TeachMeAnatomy），或大、小结节（Kenhub、StatPearls、Radiopaedia）。
- Superior glenohumeral ligament origin: the supraglenoid tubercle (Kenhub); Radiopaedia adds the coracoid. Radiopaedia also says it is present in 97% of shoulders and the middle ligament is absent in about 30%.<br>盂肱上韧带的起点：盂上结节（Kenhub）；Radiopaedia 另加喙突。Radiopaedia 还写到盂肱上韧带见于 97% 的肩关节，盂肱中韧带约 30% 缺如。
- Transverse humeral ligament: TeachMeAnatomy describes it as holding the biceps tendon; StatPearls says it may not be a distinct structure.<br>肱横韧带：TeachMeAnatomy 写它固定肱二头肌腱；StatPearls 说它可能不是一条独立的结构。
- Ranges of motion: Kenhub gives two sets. For the glenohumeral joint alone (active): flexion 110°, extension 60°, abduction 120°. For the whole shoulder complex: flexion 180°, extension 90°, abduction 180°, adduction 30°. StatPearls lists flexion 180°, extension 45–60° and abduction 150° under glenohumeral joint motion without separating out scapular motion, so its flexion matches Kenhub's whole-complex figure while abduction still differs (150° against 180°). The chapter's figure 12 splits 180° of elevation into about 120° at the glenohumeral joint and 60° at the scapula, as a teaching approximation.<br>活动范围：Kenhub 给了两组数字。仅盂肱关节（主动）：屈 110°、伸 60°、外展 120°。整个肩复合体：屈 180°、伸 90°、外展 180°、内收 30°。StatPearls 把屈 180°、伸 45–60°、外展 150° 列在“盂肱关节运动”下，没有把肩胛骨的活动分开；所以它的屈与 Kenhub 的肩复合体数字一致，外展仍有差别（150° 对 180°）。章节图 12 把 180° 上举分成盂肱关节约 120°、肩胛骨约 60°，这是教学上的概括。
- Resting position: Kenhub gives 40–50° of abduction with 30° of horizontal adduction; Lin 2007 measured a mean of 49.8° or 23.7° of abduction depending on the method.<br>休息位：Kenhub 写外展 40–50° 加水平内收 30°；Lin 2007 按测量方法不同，得到的平均外展角是 49.8° 或 23.7°。
- Rotator interval: Radiopaedia puts the lateral margin of the coracoid base at its medial side, roofs it with capsule reinforced by the CHL, and lists the long head of biceps tendon and the SGHL as its contents. StatPearls borders it with the supraspinatus tendon above, the subscapularis tendon below, the CHL laterally and the SGHL medially, and lists the long head of biceps tendon, the CHL and the SGHL as its contents. The tables and the self-test follow Radiopaedia.<br>肩袖间隙：Radiopaedia 以喙突根部外侧缘为内侧界，顶是由喙肱韧带加强的关节囊，内容为肱二头肌长头腱和盂肱上韧带。StatPearls 以冈上肌腱为上界、肩胛下肌腱为下界、喙肱韧带为外侧界、盂肱上韧带为内侧界，内容为肱二头肌长头腱、喙肱韧带和盂肱上韧带。表格和自测题按 Radiopaedia 的说法。

Why this matters clinically: most dislocations are anterior, usually from excessive extension and lateral rotation, and a torn capsule raises the risk of dislocating again (TeachMeAnatomy). The chapter adds that an anterior dislocation can involve the axillary nerve and subscapularis. In adhesive capsulitis the capsule becomes inflamed and stiff (StatPearls), which fits the capsular pattern above.

临床意义：大多数脱位是前脱位，通常由过度伸展和外旋引起；关节囊撕裂后，再次脱位的风险更高（TeachMeAnatomy）。本周章节还写到，前脱位可累及腋神经和肩胛下肌。粘连性关节囊炎时关节囊发炎、变僵硬（StatPearls），这和上面的关节囊模式相符。

> **Take-home 本章要点：** A loose capsule from the glenoid margin to the anatomical neck, thickened in front and below by three glenohumeral ligaments, reinforced above by the coracohumeral ligament, and weakest below and at the rotator interval. Because the capsule is so loose, holding the head in the glenoid depends largely on the cuff.  
> 关节囊松弛，从关节盂边缘到解剖颈；前部和下部由三条盂肱韧带加厚，上方由喙肱韧带加强；最薄弱的是下部和肩袖间隙。正因为关节囊这么松，把肱骨头稳在关节盂里很大程度要靠肩袖。
