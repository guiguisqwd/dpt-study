C stands for cervical (neck), whereas T stands for thoracic (chest). In an innervation label such as C5–C6, the numbers identify the spinal nerve levels contributing fibers to the nerve; they do not identify two thoracic vertebrae. There are seven cervical vertebrae, C1–C7, but eight pairs of cervical spinal nerves, C1–C8. C1–C7 nerves leave above the same-numbered vertebra; C8 leaves between C7 and T1. The roots of the brachial plexus are the anterior rami of spinal nerves C5–T1, not the vertebral bones themselves.

C 是 cervical（颈部）的缩写，T 是 thoracic（胸部）的缩写。神经支配中的 C5–C6 指向该神经贡献纤维的脊神经节段，不是两块胸椎。颈椎有 7 块（C1–C7），颈神经有 8 对（C1–C8）；C1–C7 神经从同名椎骨上方穿出，C8 从 C7 与 T1 之间穿出。臂丛的“根”是 C5–T1 脊神经的前支，而不是椎骨本身。

From roots to nerves: how do the suprascapular and axillary nerves leave the brachial plexus?

从根到神经：肩胛上神经和腋神经各从臂丛的哪里发出？

Compare with the reference · 对照原文

The anterior rami of C5 and C6 unite to form the superior trunk. Each trunk divides into anterior and posterior divisions. The posterior divisions contribute to the posterior cord, from which the axillary and subscapular nerves arise. The suprascapular nerve branches earlier, directly from the superior trunk.

C5、C6 脊神经的前支合成上干。各干再分为前股和后股；后股共同组成后束，腋神经和肩胛下神经从后束发出。肩胛上神经更早分出，直接起自上干。

Suprascapular nerve: its origin and levels, then its course from the neck to Infraspinatus.

肩胛上神经：起源和节段，再写它从颈部到冈下肌的走行。

Compare with the reference · 对照原文

The suprascapular nerve arises from the superior trunk of the brachial plexus, usually carrying fibers from C5 and C6. It courses posterolaterally across the posterior triangle of the neck and passes through the suprascapular notch beneath the superior transverse scapular ligament. It gives motor branches to Supraspinatus in the supraspinous fossa, then passes around the lateral end of the scapular spine through the spinoglenoid notch to supply Infraspinatus in the infraspinous fossa.

肩胛上神经起自臂丛上干，通常含 C5、C6 神经纤维。它向后外侧经过颈后三角，从肩胛上横韧带下方穿过肩胛上切迹，在冈上窝内发出支配冈上肌的运动支；随后绕过肩胛冈外侧端，经冈盂切迹进入冈下窝，支配冈下肌。

Axillary nerve: its origin and levels, the space it passes through, and what each branch supplies.

腋神经：起源和节段、穿过的间隙、两个分支各支配什么。

Compare with the reference · 对照原文

The axillary nerve arises from the posterior cord of the brachial plexus, mainly from C5 and C6. It passes posteriorly through the quadrangular space with the posterior circumflex humeral artery and winds around the surgical neck of the Humerus deep to Deltoid. Its anterior branch supplies the anterior and middle parts of Deltoid. Its posterior branch supplies Teres minor and the posterior part of Deltoid and continues as the superior lateral cutaneous nerve of the arm.

腋神经起自臂丛后束，主要含 C5、C6 神经纤维。它与旋肱后动脉一起向后穿过四边孔，在三角肌深面绕行肱骨外科颈。前支支配三角肌前部和中部；后支支配小圆肌及三角肌后部，并延续为臂外上侧皮神经。

Quadrangular space: the four boundaries, and what passes through.

四边孔：四条边界，以及从中穿过的结构。

Compare with the reference · 对照原文

The quadrangular space is a gap in the posterior axilla, bounded by the teres minor superiorly, the teres major inferiorly, the long head of the triceps brachii medially and the surgical neck of the humerus laterally. The axillary nerve passes through it with the posterior circumflex humeral artery before supplying the teres minor and the deltoid.

四边孔是腋区后部的间隙，上界为小圆肌，下界为大圆肌，内界为肱三头肌长头，外界为肱骨外科颈。腋神经与旋肱后动脉一起穿过四边孔，随后支配小圆肌和三角肌。

Compression and injury: what happens at the suprascapular notch, at the spinoglenoid notch, and when the axillary nerve is injured?

受压与损伤：在肩胛上切迹、冈盂切迹分别会怎样？腋神经损伤会怎样？

Compare with the reference · 对照原文

Entrapment at the suprascapular notch leads to atrophy of both the supraspinatus and the infraspinatus. Entrapment at the spinoglenoid notch leads to atrophy of the infraspinatus only. Axillary nerve injury: Deltoid weakness and badge-area sensory loss. Risk: a surgical-neck fracture or an anterior shoulder dislocation.

肩胛上切迹处卡压会导致冈上肌和冈下肌萎缩。冈盂切迹处卡压只导致冈下肌萎缩。腋神经损伤：三角肌无力和徽章区感觉减退。风险因素：肱骨外科颈骨折或肩关节前脱位。

Dermatome or nerve territory? Explain the badge area, then give the C5 and C6 myotomes.

皮节还是神经分布区？解释徽章区，再说出 C5、C6 的代表性肌节。

Compare with the reference · 对照原文

Regimental badge area: the skin supplied by the superior lateral cutaneous nerve of arm, a cutaneous branch of the Axillary nerve. Peripheral nerve territory ≠ dermatome: the lateral upper arm overlaps the C5 dermatome. Key myotomes: C5, shoulder abduction; C6, elbow flexion and wrist extension. These movements receive overlapping segmental contributions.

徽章区：腋神经的皮肤感觉分布，由臂外上侧皮神经（腋神经发出的皮支）支配。周围神经分布区不等同于皮节：上臂外侧区域与 C5 皮节重叠。代表性肌节：C5 肩外展；C6 屈肘和伸腕。这些动作通常接受相邻神经节段的共同支配。

Back to today's case: equal muscle bulk in both fossae and strong external rotation gave no sign of a suprascapular nerve lesion; normal deltoid strength and badge-area sensation gave no sign of an axillary nerve lesion. In this week's chapter, resisted external rotation and the external rotation lag sign are listed for Infraspinatus (suprascapular nerve), and Hornblower sign for Teres minor (axillary nerve).

回到今天的案例：两侧冈上窝、冈下窝肌肉对称，外旋有力，说明没有肩胛上神经损伤的表现；三角肌力量和徽章区感觉正常，说明没有腋神经损伤的表现。本周章节里，冈下肌（肩胛上神经）对应抗阻外旋和外旋滞后征，小圆肌（腋神经）对应 Hornblower 征。

Take-home · 本章要点
Two nerves from C5–C6, two routes: the suprascapular nerve leaves the superior trunk early and runs through two notches to two fossae; the axillary nerve leaves the posterior cord, goes through the quadrangular space and around the surgical neck to Deltoid and Teres minor.两条神经都来自 C5–C6，走两条路：肩胛上神经早早从上干分出，经两个切迹到两个窝；腋神经从后束发出，穿过四边孔、绕外科颈，到三角肌和小圆肌。
