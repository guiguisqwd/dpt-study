#### Ms Grant, 48: right shoulder pain while stocking shelves｜Grant 女士，48 岁：理货时右肩痛

- **S Subjective 主观**：Ms Grant, 48, right-handed, stacks shelves in a supermarket. Right shoulder pain for 3 weeks, starting gradually after a week of restocking high shelves; no fall or injury. The pain is over the outer upper arm (deltoid region) and does not go past the elbow. It is worse when reaching to shoulder height or overhead and when brushing her hair, and settles within about 30 minutes of rest. Worst pain 6/10. It wakes her about twice a week when she rolls onto the right side; she gets back to sleep. No neck pain, no pins and needles or numbness, no feeling that the shoulder might slip out, no sudden loss of the ability to lift the arm. No fever, weight loss or history of cancer. Her goal: full shifts including the top shelves, and sleeping on her right side.<br>Grant 女士，48 岁，右利手，在超市理货。右肩痛 3 周，是在集中补了一周高层货架之后慢慢开始的，没有摔倒或受伤。疼痛在上臂外侧（三角肌区），不过肘。往肩部高度或头顶上方够东西、梳头时加重，休息约 30 分钟后缓解。最痛 6/10。每周大约有两次翻身压到右侧时痛醒，之后能再睡着。没有颈痛，没有发麻或刺痛，没有肩要脱出的感觉，没有突然抬不起手臂。没有发烧、体重下降或癌症史。她的目标：上满班（包括高层货架），能侧躺右边睡觉。
- **O Objective 客观**：Observation: no swelling, redness or deformity; muscle bulk in the supraspinous and infraspinous fossae is the same on both sides. Neck movements are full and do not change the arm pain. Palpation: tender below the acromion (subacromial); the acromioclavicular joint is not tender. Active abduction reaches full range but is painful between about 70° and 100° (painful arc). Passive range is full and pain-free on both sides, including external rotation with the arm by the side. Resisted abduction and resisted external rotation reproduce her pain, with strength equal to the left. No external rotation lag sign. Deltoid strength and sensation over the lateral upper arm (the badge area) are normal.<br>望诊：无肿胀、发红或畸形；两侧冈上窝、冈下窝的肌肉体积一样。颈部活动全范围，不改变手臂疼痛。触诊：肩峰下方压痛；肩锁关节无压痛。主动外展能到全范围，但约 70° 到 100° 之间疼痛（疼痛弧）。两侧被动活动度都是全范围且不痛，包括手臂放在体侧时的外旋。抗阻外展和抗阻外旋诱发出她平时的疼痛，力量与左侧相同。外旋滞后征阴性。三角肌力量和上臂外侧（徽章区）感觉正常。
- **A Assessment 评估**：Working diagnosis: subacromial pain syndrome, with the supraspinatus tendon the likely source (supraspinatus tendinopathy). Naming the tendon is a hypothesis: no single clinical test can make a pathognomonic diagnosis (Hegedus 2012), and the Dutch guideline counts supraspinatus tendinopathy as one of the labels under subacromial pain syndrome (Diercks 2014). No red flags. Nothing points to a full-thickness tear, frozen shoulder, osteoarthritis, pain from the neck, or a suprascapular or axillary nerve lesion (see the table below). Low irritability: the pain settles within about 30 minutes, so she does not fit the irritable picture Lewis describes, of easily aggravated pain that lasts once provoked, with night pain (our judgement).<br>工作诊断：肩峰下疼痛综合征，最可能的来源是冈上肌腱（冈上肌腱病）。把来源定为这条肌腱只是假设：没有哪一项临床检查能单独确诊（Hegedus 2012）；荷兰指南把冈上肌腱病算作肩峰下疼痛综合征下面的一种名称（Diercks 2014）。无红旗征。没有表现指向全层撕裂、冻结肩、骨关节炎、颈源性疼痛，或肩胛上神经、腋神经损伤（见下表）。激惹性低：疼痛约 30 分钟内缓解，不符合 Lewis 描述的高激惹表现（容易诱发、诱发后持续，并有夜间痛）；这是我们的判断。
- **P Plan 计划**：1. Explain: a clinical diagnosis; no imaging is needed to start; most symptoms respond within three months (BESS 2025). 2. Activity: keep moving within comfort and avoid tasks that are too long, heavy or frequent (Dutch guideline 2026); cut back on the top shelves for now and agree a return-to-work plan with her and her employer early (Lafrance 2022). 3. Exercise: rotator cuff exercises with an eccentric emphasis and scapular stabiliser exercises, at home once or twice a day; pain while exercising up to 5/10 and back to its usual level before the next session, otherwise lower the load (Holmgren 2012). 4. Review every 2 weeks; change one thing at a time and watch the next 24 hours (Lewis 2016). 5. When the plan changes: figure 01.<br>1. 解释：这是临床诊断；开始治疗不需要影像；大多数人的症状三个月内会好转（BESS 2025）。2. 活动：在舒服的范围内继续活动，避免时间太长、太重或太频繁的动作（荷兰指南 2026）；暂时少做高层货架，尽早和她及雇主一起定返岗计划（Lafrance 2022）。3. 运动：以离心为重点的肩袖训练加肩胛稳定肌训练，在家每天 1–2 次；练习时疼痛不超过 5/10，下次练习前回落到平时水平，否则减负荷（Holmgren 2012）。4. 每 2 周复查；一次只改一项，观察之后 24 小时的反应（Lewis 2016）。5. 什么时候改方案：见图 01。

**易混点：** 夜间痛单独出现，既不能直接当成红旗，也不能直接忽略。AAFP 2008 把夜间痛列在肩袖疾病里，也提到它和肩袖撕裂有关；Delphi 共识说肩袖相关肩痛会影响睡眠（Requejo-Salinas 2022）。但颈臂痛综述把发烧、癌症史、夜间痛、体重下降一起列为恶性病变的提示（AAFP 2016）。所以问到夜间痛，要接着问发烧、体重和癌症史，看它们是不是一起出现。

The sources do not agree on how much pain is acceptable during exercise. The Dutch guideline says to stay within the pain threshold (Diercks 2014). The Holmgren trial allowed up to 5/10 if it settled before the next session. Lewis suggests that, in non-irritable presentations whose progress has plateaued, pain of about 2 to 5/10 may be tried if it settles soon after and does not increase pain at night or over 24 hours. The BESS review could not tell whether exercising below or above the pain threshold makes a clinically important difference. This case uses the Holmgren rule because that trial gives a complete protocol.

练习时能接受多少疼痛，来源之间不一致。荷兰指南说要在疼痛阈值以内（Diercks 2014）。Holmgren 试验允许到 5/10，前提是下次练习前能回落。Lewis 认为，低激惹且进展停滞的病人可以尝试约 2–5/10 的疼痛，但练后要很快缓解，并且不加重夜间痛和 24 小时内的疼痛。BESS 的证据回顾无法判断在疼痛阈值以下还是以上练习有没有临床上重要的差别。本例采用 Holmgren 的规定，因为这项试验给出了完整的方案。

No source opened for this pack gives numeric criteria for returning to overhead work. The case therefore uses criteria agreed with the patient: full shifts including the top shelves, with pain at work no higher than during exercise and no worse the next morning. These are a teaching choice, not a published standard. What the sources do say is to build a return-to-work plan early, with the worker and other stakeholders (Lafrance 2022), and to build up exercise within sport and work gradually, to raise the load the cuff can bear (Dutch guideline 2026).

本包打开过的来源都没有给出回到过头工作的量化标准。所以本例用和病人商定的标准：上满班（包括高层货架），工作时的疼痛不超过练习时，第二天早上不加重。这是教学上的选择，不是公开发表的标准。来源给出的是做法：尽早和劳动者及相关各方一起定返岗计划（Lafrance 2022）；在运动和工作中逐步加量，提高肩袖能承受的负荷（荷兰指南 2026）。

### Speaking practice 1: taking the history｜口语练习 1：问诊

Play each line, then say it yourself. The questions follow the order of the S line: where, how it started, what changes it, night, neck and nerves, instability, red flags, goal. The key terms at the top of this chapter show the IPA and stress of the hard words.

先播放每一句，再自己说一遍。问题按 S 一栏的顺序：部位、起病、加重和缓解因素、夜间、颈部和神经、不稳、红旗征、目标。难词的音标和重音见本章开头的关键词。

- **Physio · 治疗师**：Hi, I'm Alex, one of the physiotherapists. What brings you in today?<br>你好，我是 Alex，这里的物理治疗师。今天是哪里不舒服？
- **Patient · 病人**：My right shoulder has been hurting for about three weeks.<br>我右肩痛了大概三周。
- **Physio · 治疗师**：Can you point to where you feel it most?<br>能指一下最痛的地方吗？
- **Patient · 病人**：Here, on the outside of my upper arm.<br>这里，上臂外侧。
- **Physio · 治疗师**：Does the pain ever go below your elbow?<br>疼痛会传到肘部以下吗？
- **Patient · 病人**：No, it stops above the elbow.<br>不会，到肘上面就停了。
- **Physio · 治疗师**：How did it start? Did you fall or hurt it?<br>是怎么开始的？有没有摔倒或受伤？

- **Physio · 治疗师**：Does it wake you at night?<br>晚上会痛醒吗？
- **Patient · 病人**：Sometimes, when I roll onto that side. Then I fall back asleep.<br>有时候翻身压到那边会醒，然后能再睡着。

- **Physio · 治疗师**：Has your shoulder ever felt like it might slip out?<br>肩膀有没有过要脱出来的感觉？
- **Patient · 病人**：No, never.<br>没有，从来没有。
- **Physio · 治疗师**：Have you had a fever, lost weight without trying, or ever been treated for cancer?<br>最近有没有发烧、没刻意减重却瘦了，或者以前治疗过癌症？
- **Patient · 病人**：No, I feel fine otherwise.<br>没有，别的都挺好。
- **Physio · 治疗师**：What would you most like to get back to?<br>你最想恢复做什么？
- **Patient · 病人**：Full shifts at work, and sleeping on my right side.<br>上满班，还有能侧躺右边睡觉。

### Speaking practice 2: explaining to the patient｜口语练习 2：给病人解释

- **Physio · 治疗师**：From what you've told me and what I've found, this looks like an irritated tendon at the top of your shoulder, the supraspinatus tendon.<br>根据你说的和我检查到的，这看起来是肩膀上方的一条肌腱受了刺激，叫冈上肌腱。
- **Physio · 治疗师**：It runs under the bony tip of your shoulder, the acromion, and attaches to the top of your arm bone. It is often sore in the middle of lifting the arm to the side, as yours is.<br>这条肌腱从肩膀外侧那块骨头（肩峰）下面经过，止于上臂骨的上端。手臂向侧面抬到中段时常会痛，你就是这样。
- **Patient · 病人**：Do I need a scan?<br>我需要拍片子或做扫描吗？
- **Physio · 治疗师**：Not right now. This is diagnosed from your story and the examination, and a scan isn't needed to start treatment. If things change, we'll look at it again.<br>现在不需要。这个靠病史和查体诊断，开始治疗不需要影像。情况有变化，我们再考虑。
- **Patient · 病人**：Should I stop using the arm?<br>我是不是应该先别用这只手？
- **Physio · 治疗师**：No. Keep moving it within what feels comfortable, but cut back on the top shelves for now. We'll build them back in step by step.<br>不用。在舒服的范围里继续活动，但暂时少做高层货架的活，我们再一步步加回来。
- **Physio · 治疗师**：The main treatment is exercise for the shoulder muscles. Some pain while you exercise is fine, up to five out of ten, as long as it settles back to your usual level before the next session.<br>主要的治疗是肩部肌肉的锻炼。练的时候有点痛没关系，最多到 10 分里的 5 分，只要下次练之前能回到平时的水平就行。
- **Patient · 病人**：How long will it take?<br>要多久能好？
- **Physio · 治疗师**：Most people with this problem improve within about three months. We'll check every two weeks, and if it isn't improving, we'll change the plan.<br>有这种问题的人，大多数三个月左右会好转。我们每两周复查一次，如果没有好转，就调整方案。
- **Physio · 治疗师**：Can you tell me in your own words what we're going to do?<br>你能用自己的话说说我们接下来要做什么吗？

The last line checks understanding: the patient repeats the plan in their own words, and you correct anything that is missing.

最后一句是确认病人听懂了：让病人用自己的话复述计划，漏了什么再补上。

### Speaking practice 3: reporting to a colleague｜口语练习 3：向同事汇报

- **Physio · 治疗师**：Can I run a new patient past you?<br>我跟你说一下一位新病人，可以吗？
- **Colleague · 同事**：Sure, go ahead.<br>好，你说。
- **Physio · 治疗师**：She's a 48-year-old shelf-stacker with three weeks of right lateral arm pain, gradual onset, no trauma. It's worse with overhead reaching, it doesn't go past the elbow, and she has some night pain lying on that side.<br>48 岁女性，超市理货员，右上臂外侧痛三周，逐渐起病，没有外伤。往上够东西时加重，不过肘，压着那一侧睡时夜里会痛。
- **Physio · 治疗师**：Her neck is clear, no neuro symptoms, no red flags and no instability.<br>颈椎问题已排除，没有神经症状，没有红旗征，没有不稳。
- **Physio · 治疗师**：She has a painful arc from about seventy to a hundred degrees, subacromial tenderness and full passive range, including external rotation. Resisted abduction and external rotation are painful but strong, with no lag sign.<br>疼痛弧大约在 70° 到 100°，肩峰下压痛，被动活动度全范围，包括外旋。抗阻外展和抗阻外旋会痛，但力量正常，没有滞后征。
- **Colleague · 同事**：Any wasting over the scapula?<br>肩胛骨那里有没有肌肉萎缩？
- **Physio · 治疗师**：None. Bulk is equal in both fossae, and deltoid strength and sensation over the badge area are normal.<br>没有。冈上窝、冈下窝肌肉对称，三角肌力量和徽章区感觉都正常。
- **Physio · 治疗师**：My working diagnosis is subacromial pain syndrome, most likely supraspinatus tendinopathy, with low irritability.<br>我的工作诊断是肩峰下疼痛综合征，最可能是冈上肌腱病，激惹性低。
- **Physio · 治疗师**：Plan: education, cuff and scapular loading at home with pain up to five out of ten, and a review in two weeks. If there's no improvement by six to eight weeks, we'll discuss an injection with her GP.<br>计划：宣教，在家做肩袖和肩胛稳定肌的负荷训练，疼痛不超过 5/10，两周后复查。6–8 周仍无改善，就和她的家庭医生商量注射。
- **Colleague · 同事**：Sounds reasonable. Let me know how she's doing at the review.<br>听起来合理。复查时告诉我她的情况。

A report runs in the same order as the note: who the patient is and the main complaint, what was ruled out, the key findings, the working diagnosis, then the plan and when it will be reviewed.

汇报的顺序和记录一样：病人是谁、主诉，排除了什么，关键体征，工作诊断，最后是计划和复查时间。

#### Check yourself｜先自测

**Q.** Which findings in this case argue against frozen shoulder, and which against a full-thickness tear?<br>这个案例里哪些表现不支持冻结肩？哪些不支持全层撕裂？

<details><summary>Answer 答案</summary>

Against frozen shoulder: full passive range, including passive external rotation. Against a full-thickness tear: normal strength on resisted testing, no lag sign, and no sudden loss of active elevation.<br>不支持冻结肩：被动活动度全范围，包括被动外旋。不支持全层撕裂：抗阻测试力量正常，没有滞后征，没有突然不能主动抬臂。

</details>

**Q.** Why is supraspinatus tendinopathy written as a working diagnosis rather than a certainty?<br>为什么冈上肌腱病写成工作诊断，而不是确定的诊断？

<details><summary>Answer 答案</summary>

No single clinical test can make a pathognomonic diagnosis (Hegedus 2012). The findings fit the supraspinatus tendon, but the examination cannot prove which structure is painful.<br>没有哪一项临床检查能单独确诊（Hegedus 2012）。这些表现符合冈上肌腱，但查体证明不了到底是哪个结构在痛。

</details>

**Q.** What is the rule for pain during exercise in this plan, and where does it come from?<br>这个计划里练习时的疼痛怎么规定？出自哪里？

<details><summary>Answer 答案</summary>

Up to 5/10 while exercising, and back to the usual level before the next session; otherwise the load is lowered. It is the Holmgren 2012 trial's rule; other sources set it differently.<br>练习时不超过 5/10，下次练习前要回到平时水平，否则减负荷。这是 Holmgren 2012 试验的规定；其他来源的规定不一样。

</details>

**Q.** At which two points does the plan change if she is not improving?<br>如果她没有好转，计划在哪两个时间点改变？

<details><summary>Answer 答案</summary>

By 6–8 weeks: one subacromial steroid injection can be considered in a shared decision. By 12 weeks of non-operative care: referral to secondary care (BESS 2025).<br>6–8 周：可以共同决定是否做一次肩峰下类固醇注射。保守治疗满 12 周：转专科（BESS 2025）。

</details>

**Q.** Which nerve checks are in the O line, and which nerve does each test?<br>O 一栏里有哪些神经检查？各查的是哪条神经？

<details><summary>Answer 答案</summary>

Muscle bulk in the supraspinous and infraspinous fossae: suprascapular nerve. Deltoid strength and badge-area sensation: axillary nerve. External rotation strength involves both: Infraspinatus (suprascapular nerve) and Teres minor (axillary nerve).<br>冈上窝、冈下窝的肌肉体积：肩胛上神经。三角肌力量和徽章区感觉：腋神经。外旋力量两条都涉及：冈下肌（肩胛上神经）和小圆肌（腋神经）。

</details>


> **Take-home 本章要点：** A case note is a chain of reasons: each finding either supports the working diagnosis or rules something else out, and the plan says in advance when it will change.  
> 病例记录是一串理由：每个表现要么支持工作诊断，要么排除别的可能；计划要事先写明什么时候改。
