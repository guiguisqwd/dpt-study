Spaced review brings each card back 1, 3, 7, 14 and 30 days after the day it was first learned, and it runs across weeks. The cards are the acupoints and muscles of earlier packs; this week only Monday added new cards: SI14, SI15, TE15 and LI14.

间隔复习让每张卡在初次学习后第 1、3、7、14、30 天再出现，并且跨周进行。卡片是之前各包里的穴位和肌肉；本周只有周一加了新卡：肩外俞、肩中俞、天髎、臂臑。
