**Q.** Choose one: with the hand on the belly and the arm turned fully in, the patient cannot hold the position and the elbow drops back behind the trunk. Which muscle does this point to? (a) supraspinatus (b) infraspinatus (c) teres minor (d) subscapularis<br>单选：手放在腹部、手臂充分内旋，病人保持不住，肘部向后落到躯干后方。这提示哪块肌肉？（a）冈上肌（b）冈下肌（c）小圆肌（d）肩胛下肌

<details><summary>Answer 答案</summary>

(d) Subscapularis: a positive belly-press test. In Barth 2006, with arthroscopy as the reference, the test was 40% sensitive and 97.9% specific. Barth also counted weakness against the other side, or pressing by extending the elbow or shoulder, as positive, so these numbers are not for the elbow drop alone. Still, a positive result says much more than a negative one, which does not rule a tear out. (Friday)<br>（d）肩胛下肌：腹压试验阳性。Barth 2006 以关节镜为参考标准，敏感度 40%，特异度 97.9%；Barth 的阳性标准还包括与对侧相比无力，或靠伸肘、伸肩按压腹部，所以这组数字不是只按“肘部后落”判读的。阳性结果的分量仍远大于阴性结果，阴性不能排除撕裂。（周五）

</details>

**Q.** Choose one: Calis 2000 found the Hawkins-Kennedy test 92.1% sensitive and 25.0% specific. Which statement is right? (a) A positive result confirms subacromial pain. (b) A negative result lowers the probability somewhat but does not rule it out. (c) The result means nothing either way. (d) A specificity of 25% means that a quarter of positive results are true.<br>单选：Calis 2000 发现 Hawkins-Kennedy 试验敏感度 92.1%、特异度 25.0%。哪一句对？（a）阳性结果可以确诊肩峰下疼痛。（b）阴性结果能让可能性降低一些，但不能排除。（c）结果无论阴阳都没有意义。（d）特异度 25% 表示四分之一的阳性结果是真的。

<details><summary>Answer 答案</summary>

(b). High sensitivity means few people with the condition test negative, so a negative result lowers the probability; but in the same study 44% of the shoulders with a negative Hawkins test still had a positive injection test. Low specificity means many people without the condition test positive, so a positive result adds little. Option (d) confuses specificity with the share of positive results that are true. (Friday)<br>（b）。敏感度高，说明有病的人很少测成阴性，所以阴性结果能降低可能性；但同一研究里，Hawkins 阴性的肩膀仍有 44% 注射试验阳性。特异度低，说明没病的人很多也测成阳性，所以阳性结果帮助不大。（d）把特异度和“阳性结果中真阳性所占的比例”混为一谈。（周五）

</details>

(b). Plain X-rays are essential to confirm osteoarthritis; in frozen shoulder they should be normal. (Tuesday)<br>（b）。确认骨关节炎必须拍 X 线平片；冻结肩的 X 线应当正常。肩峰下疼痛通常被动活动度完整；肩袖病变常常是主动活动度丢失，而被动活动度相对保留。（周二）

### Part 4 · One patient, the whole week｜第 4 部分 · 一个病人串起一周

Wednesday's teaching case: Ms Grant, 48, stacks supermarket shelves. Her right shoulder pain came on gradually three weeks ago, after a week of restocking high shelves; there was no fall or injury. She has a painful arc between about 70° and 100° and full, pain-free passive range. Resisted abduction and external rotation are painful but as strong as on the left. There is no external rotation lag sign, and deltoid strength and badge-area sensation are normal.

#### Check yourself｜先自测

**Q.** Her external rotation lag sign is negative. Castoldi 2009 found the sign 56% sensitive and 98% specific for an isolated full-thickness supraspinatus tear. How much does the negative result reassure you on its own, and what makes a full-thickness tear unlikely in her case?<br>她的外旋滞后征阴性。Castoldi 2009 发现这项体征对单纯冈上肌全层撕裂的敏感度 56%、特异度 98%。单凭这个阴性结果能让你放心多少？她的哪些表现让全层撕裂不太可能？

<details><summary>Answer 答案</summary>

Not much on its own: with 56% sensitivity, nearly half of the people who have such a tear still test negative. What argues against a full-thickness tear is the whole picture: normal strength on resisted testing, no lag sign and no sudden loss of active elevation. (Wednesday; Friday)<br>单凭它放心不了多少：敏感度 56%，有这种撕裂的人将近一半仍测成阴性。让全层撕裂不太可能的是整体表现：抗阻测试力量正常、没有滞后征、没有突然失去主动上举能力。（周三；周五）

</details>

**Q.** Why can her care start without imaging, and which two findings would have needed same-day referral instead?<br>为什么她可以不做影像检查就开始治疗？哪两种表现则需要当天转诊？

<details><summary>Answer 答案</summary>

Subacromial pain syndrome is a clinical diagnosis, no imaging is needed to start, and most symptoms respond within three months (BESS 2025). Same-day referral: an acutely painful, red, warm joint (possible infection), or trauma followed by loss of rotation and an abnormal shape (an unreduced dislocation) (BESS/BOA 2014). (Tuesday; Wednesday)<br>肩峰下疼痛综合征是临床诊断，开始治疗不需要影像，多数症状在三个月内好转（BESS 2025）。需要当天转诊的是：急性疼痛、红、热的关节（可能感染），或外伤后旋转丧失、外形异常（未复位的脱位）（BESS/BOA 2014）。（周二；周三）

</details>
