(d) Subscapularis: a positive belly-press test. In Barth 2006, with arthroscopy as the reference, the test was 40% sensitive and 97.9% specific. Barth also counted weakness against the other side, or pressing by extending the elbow or shoulder, as positive, so these numbers are not for the elbow drop alone. Still, a positive result says much more than a negative one, which does not rule a tear out. (Friday)

（d）肩胛下肌：腹压试验阳性。Barth 2006 以关节镜为参考标准，敏感度 40%，特异度 97.9%；Barth 的阳性标准还包括与对侧相比无力，或靠伸肘、伸肩按压腹部，所以这组数字不是只按“肘部后落”判读的。阳性结果的分量仍远大于阴性结果，阴性不能排除撕裂。（周五）

(b). High sensitivity means few people with the condition test negative, so a negative result lowers the probability; but in the same study 44% of the shoulders with a negative Hawkins test still had a positive injection test. Low specificity means many people without the condition test positive, so a positive result adds little. Option (d) confuses specificity with the share of positive results that are true. (Friday)

（b）。敏感度高，说明有病的人很少测成阴性，所以阴性结果能降低可能性；但同一研究里，Hawkins 阴性的肩膀仍有 44% 注射试验阳性。特异度低，说明没病的人很多也测成阳性，所以阳性结果帮助不大。（d）把特异度和“阳性结果中真阳性所占的比例”混为一谈。（周五）

Not much on its own: with 56% sensitivity, nearly half of the people who have such a tear still test negative. What argues against a full-thickness tear is the whole picture: normal strength on resisted testing, no lag sign and no sudden loss of active elevation. (Wednesday; Friday)

Subacromial pain syndrome is a clinical diagnosis, no imaging is needed to start, and most symptoms respond within three months (BESS 2025). Same-day referral: an acutely painful, red, warm joint (possible infection), or trauma followed by loss of rotation and an abnormal shape (an unreduced dislocation) (BESS/BOA 2014).
