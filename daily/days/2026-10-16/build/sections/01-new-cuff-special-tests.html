Tuesday's pathway said that a combination of the Hawkins-Kennedy, Neer, Jobe and external rotation resistance tests is useful (BESS 2025), and that no single test can make the diagnosis on its own (Hegedus 2012); it promised a Friday chapter on how each test is done. This is that chapter, though resisted external rotation is not covered here: the external rotation lag sign below is a different test. This week's chapter pairs each cuff muscle with its tests, and today takes one from each row: the empty can for supraspinatus (the chapter also lists the drop arm test), the external rotation lag sign for infraspinatus (also resisted external rotation), the hornblower sign for teres minor, and the belly-press test for subscapularis (also the lift-off test). First, how to read the numbers先看懂数字

Sensitivity is the share of people who have the target condition and test positive. Specificity is the share of people without it who test negative. A highly sensitive test seldom misses the condition, so a negative result helps to rule it out; a highly specific test seldom flags people who do not have it, so a positive result helps to rule it in. Both numbers belong to one study. They depend on the target condition (a full-thickness tear is not the same thing as subacromial pain), on the reference standard that decided who really had it (MRI, surgery, or a local anaesthetic injection), and on which patients were examined. That is why every number below comes with its study and target, and why you should compare a test's sensitivity with its own specificity, not with another study's numbers.

敏感度是真正有目标疾病的人里，检查呈阳性的比例。特异度是没有这个病的人里，检查呈阴性的比例。敏感度很高的检查很少漏掉病人，所以阴性结果有助于排除；特异度很高的检查很少把没病的人判成阳性，所以阳性结果有助于确认。两个数字都属于某一项研究，取决于目标疾病（全层撕裂和肩峰下疼痛不是一回事）、用来判定谁真有病的参考标准（MRI、手术或局麻药注射），以及检查了哪些病人。所以下面每个数字都带着研究和目标疾病；比较时拿同一项研究里的敏感度和特异度相比，不要拿不同研究的数字互比。

Supraspinatus: empty can and full can冈上肌：空罐试验与满罐试验

Empty can (Jobe test): the arms are raised to 90° in the scapular plane, about 30° forward of the coronal plane, and fully internally rotated so that the thumbs point to the floor. The examiner pushes the arms down and the patient resists (Barnes; Itoi 1999 as described by Katepun 2023). Full can: the same 90° of elevation in the scapular plane, but with about 45° of external rotation (Itoi 1999 as described by Katepun 2023), so that the thumbs point up (Nicolao 2022). Kelly and colleagues proposed this position after recording muscle activity in 11 shoulders: supraspinatus was best isolated at 90° of scapular-plane elevation with 45° of external rotation (Kelly 1996). Test both sides and compare.

空罐试验（Jobe 试验）：双臂在肩胛平面（冠状面前方约 30°）抬到 90°，充分内旋，拇指朝向地面。检查者向下压手臂，病人对抗（Barnes；Katepun 2023 转述 Itoi 1999 的做法）。满罐试验：同样在肩胛平面抬到 90°，但外旋约 45°（Katepun 2023 转述 Itoi 1999 的做法），拇指朝上（Nicolao 2022）。Kelly 等在 11 个肩上记录肌电后提出这个体位：肩胛平面抬高 90°、外旋 45° 时，冈上肌最容易被单独检查出来（Kelly 1996）。两侧都做，左右比较。

What counts as positive: pain or weakness during the manoeuvre (Barnes). Itoi and colleagues scored each test three ways in 143 shoulders, against MRI, looking for a full-thickness supraspinatus tear: pain, weakness, or pain or weakness or both. Weakness was the most accurate reading for both tests: 75% accurate for the full can and 70% for the empty can, a difference that was not significant. Pain came on in 43% of shoulders during the full can and 50% during the empty can, a difference that was also not statistically significant; even so, because the full can provoked less pain, the authors suggested it may be the more useful in practice (Itoi 1999). Read for weakness, the empty can had a sensitivity of 77% and a specificity of 68%, and the full can 77% and 74% (Itoi 1999, as tabulated by Dinnes 2003).

怎样算阳性：检查中出现疼痛或无力（Barnes）。Itoi 等在 143 个肩上以 MRI 为参考标准、以冈上肌腱全层撕裂为目标，把每项检查按三种方式判读：疼痛、无力、疼痛或无力（任一项或两项都有）。两项检查都是按“无力”判读最准：满罐准确率 75%，空罐 70%，差别没有统计学意义。满罐有 43% 的肩出现疼痛，空罐有 50%，这一差别同样没有统计学意义；尽管如此，作者考虑到满罐诱发疼痛较少，认为它在临床上可能更好用（Itoi 1999）。按“无力”判读时，空罐敏感度 77%、特异度 68%，满罐 77% 和 74%（Itoi 1999，Dinnes 2003 汇总表）。

A second study gives a different picture because it asked a different question. The ROW cohort examined patients older than 45 with shoulder pain for at least 4 weeks and listed these two tests under supraspinatus tears. A tear counted only when blinded MRI review showed one and the attending physician rated their certainty that the symptoms came from a cuff tear at 50 or more out of 100. There Jobe's test had a sensitivity of 88% and a specificity of 62%, and the full can 70% and 81% (Jain 2017).

另一项研究的数字不一样，因为它问的问题不一样。ROW 队列纳入 45 岁以上、肩痛至少 4 周的病人，把这两项检查列在冈上肌撕裂之下。只有盲法阅片的 MRI 显示有撕裂、且主治医生对“症状由肩袖撕裂引起”的把握评分在 100 分中达到 50 分以上，才算有撕裂。这项研究里 Jobe 试验敏感度 88%、特异度 62%，满罐 70% 和 81%（Jain 2017）。

易混点：空罐、满罐的“阳性”要先说清楚按什么判：疼痛还是无力。Itoi 1999 发现判断有没有全层撕裂时，“无力”比“疼痛”准；Barnes 指出，阳性也可能提示合并的肩峰下撞击，所以单有疼痛不等于撕裂。周二那一章引用 AAFP 2008 说过：空罐时患侧比健侧无力，可能是肌腱病，也可能是撕裂。

Pain under the acromion: Neer and Hawkins-Kennedy肩峰下疼痛：Neer 试验与 Hawkins-Kennedy 试验

Neer sign: the patient sits. With one hand the examiner presses down on the clavicle and acromion so that the scapula cannot move; with the other the examiner forcibly elevates the arm in the scapular plane. Pain is a positive sign. Neer test is a second step: about 10 ml of 1% lidocaine is injected under the anterior acromion and the manoeuvre is repeated; if the injection abolishes the pain, the Neer test is positive (Cochrane review, Hanchard 2013, Table 1, describing Neer 1977 and 1983). The plane is not described the same way everywhere: Calis 2000 elevated the arm “at an angle between flexion and abduction”.

Neer 征：病人坐位。检查者一只手向下按住锁骨和肩峰，不让肩胛骨动；另一只手在肩胛平面用力把手臂抬起来。出现疼痛为阳性。Neer 试验是第二步：在肩峰前部下方注射约 10 ml 1% 利多卡因后重复这个动作；如果注射后疼痛消失，Neer 试验为阳性（Cochrane 系统综述 Hanchard 2013 表 1，引自 Neer 1977 和 1983）。上举平面各处写法不同：Calis 2000 写的是“在屈曲与外展之间的角度”上举。

Hawkins-Kennedy test: with the patient upright, the examiner passively places the shoulder and elbow at 90° of flexion, then forcibly rotates the shoulder inward (internal rotation). Reproduction of the patient's own pain is positive (Hanchard 2013, Table 1, describing Hawkins 1980). The 1980 abstract itself describes the idea behind such signs: forcibly flexing the arm forward so that the greater tuberosity is jammed against the anteroinferior surface of the acromion. It does not mention 90° of flexion, the bent elbow or internal rotation (Hawkins 1980, abstract; the full paper was not opened).

Hawkins-Kennedy 试验：病人坐直或站立，检查者被动把肩和肘都放到屈曲 90°，然后用力把肩向内旋转（内旋）。复现病人平时的疼痛为阳性（Hanchard 2013 表 1，引自 Hawkins 1980）。1980 年的论文摘要讲了这类体征背后的想法：用力把手臂向前屈，让大结节挤向肩峰前下面；摘要没有提到屈曲 90°、屈肘或内旋（Hawkins 1980 摘要；全文没有打开）。

How accurate: both tests are more sensitive than specific. In 125 painful shoulders, with a subacromial injection as the reference standard, Hawkins had a sensitivity of 92.1% and a specificity of 25.0%, and Neer 88.7% and 30.5% (Calis 2000). Pooled across studies of subacromial impingement, Neer had a sensitivity of 72% and a specificity of 60%, and Hawkins-Kennedy 79% and 59% (Hegedus 2012). So a positive result adds little. A negative result lowers the probability only somewhat: Hegedus 2012 judged the negative likelihood ratio of Hawkins-Kennedy poor, with little effect on ruling impingement out, and in Calis 2000 about half of the shoulders with a negative Neer or Hawkins test (48% and 44%) still had a positive injection test. An earlier pooled analysis found that neither test, on its own, had diagnostic value for impingement (Hegedus 2008).

有多准：两项检查都是敏感度高于特异度。在 125 个疼痛的肩上，以肩峰下注射试验为参考标准，Hawkins 敏感度 92.1%、特异度 25.0%，Neer 88.7% 和 30.5%（Calis 2000）。把多项肩峰下撞击研究合并，Neer 敏感度 72%、特异度 60%，Hawkins-Kennedy 79% 和 59%（Hegedus 2012）。所以阳性结果帮不了多少。阴性结果也只能让可能性降低一些：Hegedus 2012 认为 Hawkins-Kennedy 的阴性似然比不好，阴性时对排除肩峰下撞击作用不大；在 Calis 2000 中，Neer 或 Hawkins 阴性的肩里仍有约一半（48% 和 44%）注射试验阳性。更早的一项合并分析认为，这两项检查单独使用都没有诊断价值（Hegedus 2008）。

易混点：“Neer 征”和“Neer 试验”不是一回事：征是做动作时痛，试验还要加上肩峰下注射后疼痛消失。Cochrane 综述还列出，Neer 征阳性也见于关节僵硬、不稳、关节炎、钙化沉积和骨病变，所以单凭它不能说就是撞击（Hanchard 2013 表 1）。

The back of the cuff: external rotation lag sign and hornblower sign肩袖后部：外旋滞后征与 Hornblower 征

External rotation lag sign (Hertel 1996): the patient sits and the examiner stands behind. The examiner supports the arm with the elbow bent to 90° and the shoulder raised 20° in the scapular plane, and turns the shoulder out to 5° short of full external rotation. The patient is asked to hold that position; the examiner lets go of the wrist but keeps supporting the elbow (Cochrane review, Hanchard 2013, Table 1, describing Hertel 1996). Collin 2015 describes the same steps and explains the 5°: it allows for the elastic recoil of the tissues. Positive: the forearm drifts back inward, toward the belly; the angle it loses is the lag. In the Cochrane table's account of Hertel, a lag of 5–10° signifies a complete tear of supraspinatus or infraspinatus, and 10–15° a complete tear of both, or a nerve problem. Hertel's own paper could not be opened, so its accuracy figures are not given here.

外旋滞后征（Hertel 1996）：病人坐位，检查者站在身后。检查者托住手臂，屈肘 90°，肩在肩胛平面抬高 20°，把肩外旋到比最大外旋少 5° 的位置。让病人保持住；检查者松开手腕，但继续托住肘部（Cochrane 系统综述 Hanchard 2013 表 1，引自 Hertel 1996）。Collin 2015 写的步骤相同，并解释了少 5° 的原因：留出组织弹性回弹的余地。阳性：前臂向内、往腹部方向回漂，回漂的角度就是“滞后”。按 Cochrane 表对 Hertel 的转述，滞后 5–10° 提示冈上肌或冈下肌完全撕裂，10–15° 提示两者都完全撕裂，或者有神经问题。Hertel 原文没能打开，所以这里不写它的准确性数字。

How accurate: the lag sign is specific but often insensitive, and the numbers depend on the target. In 401 consecutive patients (406 painful shoulders), checked at arthroscopy or open surgery, it had a sensitivity of 56% and a specificity of 98% for an isolated full-thickness supraspinatus tear; sensitivity rose substantially when infraspinatus and teres minor were also torn, and the lag grew from 7° for an isolated supraspinatus tear to 26° when the tear reached teres minor (Castoldi 2009, abstract). In 37 referred patients with ultrasound as the reference, it had a sensitivity of 46% and a specificity of 94% for full-thickness supraspinatus and infraspinatus tears; the authors stress that a negative result does not rule a tear out (Miller 2008, abstract). In the ROW cohort, looking for an infraspinatus tear, the version labelled “lag sign at 0°” (the paper does not describe the position) was 10% sensitive and 98% specific, and 21% and 98% for tears of 2 cm or more; the version at 90° was 8% and 100% (Jain 2017). The cut-off matters too: in 100 patients with massive tears, looking for severe fatty infiltration of teres minor, a lag of more than 10° gave 100% and 51%, and a lag of more than 40° gave 100% and 92% (Collin 2015).

有多准：滞后征特异度高，但常常不够敏感，而且数字随目标疾病变化。在 401 名连续就诊的病人（406 个疼痛的肩）中，以关节镜或开放手术为准，它对单纯冈上肌腱全层撕裂的敏感度 56%、特异度 98%；撕裂累及冈下肌和小圆肌时，敏感度明显升高；滞后角度从单纯冈上肌撕裂的 7° 增大到累及小圆肌时的 26°（Castoldi 2009 摘要）。在 37 名转诊病人中，以超声为参考标准，它对冈上肌和冈下肌全层撕裂的敏感度 46%、特异度 94%；作者强调阴性结果不能排除撕裂（Miller 2008 摘要）。ROW 队列以冈下肌撕裂为目标时，论文称为“0° 外旋滞后征”的做法（论文没有写具体体位）敏感度 10%、特异度 98%，撕裂 2 cm 以上时为 21% 和 98%；“90° 外旋滞后征”为 8% 和 100%（Jain 2017）。阈值也有影响：在 100 名巨大撕裂的病人中，以小圆肌严重脂肪变性为目标，滞后超过 10° 算阳性时为 100% 和 51%，超过 40° 才算阳性时为 100% 和 92%（Collin 2015）。

Hornblower sign (Walch 1998): Walch's full paper is behind a paywall, so the position comes from two secondary descriptions, which agree on the start but word the positive differently. A Korean Shoulder and Elbow Society course handout, citing Patte 1988: the examiner supports the arm at 90° of abduction in the scapular plane, the elbow is bent to 90°, and the patient tries to turn the forearm up into external rotation against the examiner's hand; positive if the shoulder cannot be turned out (Park, course handout). A 2016 review has the patient hold full external rotation in that position; positive if the arm cannot hold it and falls back into internal rotation (Moulton 2016). Collin 2015 uses the same starting position under the name 'Patte test' and grades it by strength, positive below grade 4 of 5; it does not call it the hornblower sign, so its numbers are left out here.

Hornblower 征（Walch 1998）：Walch 原文需付费，没能打开，所以体位取自两份二手描述；两者起始体位一致，阳性的说法不同。一份韩国肩肘学会的课程讲义（引用 Patte 1988）：检查者托住手臂，肩胛平面外展 90°，屈肘 90°，病人对抗检查者的手，尽力把前臂向上转（外旋）；转不出去为阳性（Park 课程讲义）。一篇 2016 年的综述写的是让病人在这个体位保持最大外旋；保持不住、手臂落回内旋为阳性（Moulton 2016）。Collin 2015 用同一个起始体位，但叫它“Patte 试验”，按肌力分级判读，低于 5 级中的 4 级为阳性；它没有称之为 Hornblower 征，所以这里不引用它的数字。

How accurate: Walch examined 54 patients operated on for combined supraspinatus and infraspinatus tears. The target was stage 3 or 4 fatty degeneration of teres minor (Goutallier grading), which the authors called irreparable; against it the hornblower sign had a sensitivity of 100% and a specificity of 93% (Walch 1998, abstract). A 2016 review quotes these figures as 94% and 100%; we use the abstract's numbers. In the ROW cohort, looking for an infraspinatus tear in patients over 45 with shoulder pain, the hornblower sign had a sensitivity of 17% and a specificity of 96% (Jain 2017). The two results differ because the questions differ: Walch looked for severe fatty degeneration of teres minor in people who already had combined supraspinatus and infraspinatus tears; ROW looked for any infraspinatus tear in a general group of painful shoulders.

有多准：Walch 研究了 54 名因冈上肌和冈下肌联合撕裂接受手术的病人。目标是小圆肌 3 或 4 期脂肪变性（Goutallier 分期），作者称之为不可修复；以此为目标，Hornblower 征敏感度 100%、特异度 93%（Walch 1998 摘要）。一篇 2016 年的综述把这两个数字写成 94% 和 100%；本文采用摘要里的数字。ROW 队列在 45 岁以上肩痛病人中以冈下肌撕裂为目标，Hornblower 征敏感度 17%、特异度 96%（Jain 2017）。两组结果差得远，是因为问的问题不同：Walch 是在已经有冈上肌和冈下肌联合撕裂的人里找严重脂肪变性的小圆肌；ROW 是在一般肩痛病人里找任何冈下肌撕裂。

易混点：滞后征和抗阻肌力检查不一样：滞后征是检查者把手臂摆到位，病人只需要保持住；抗阻检查是病人对抗检查者用力。引用滞后征的数字要连阈值一起说：同一批病人，滞后超过 10° 算阳性时特异度 51%，超过 40° 才算阳性时升到 92%（Collin 2015）。另外，Hornblower 征和 Collin 的 Patte 试验起始体位相同，名字不同，数字不要混用。

Subscapularis: the belly-press test肩胛下肌：腹压试验

Gerber and colleagues described the belly-press in 1996 for patients whose internal rotation is too limited for the lift-off test, which needs the hand behind the back. In their words, as quoted in a 2003 review: the patient presses the abdomen with the flat of the hand and tries to keep the arm in maximal internal rotation. If internal rotation is strong, the elbow does not drop backward and stays in front of the trunk. If subscapularis is weak, maximal internal rotation cannot be held, the patient feels weak, and the elbow drops back behind the trunk; the patient then presses on the abdomen by extending the shoulder rather than rotating it inward (Gerber 1996, quoted by Tennent 2003). Later authors add details the quotation does not give. Barth 2006 tested with the arm at the side and the elbow bent to 90°. Tokish 2003 started with the palm on the upper abdomen just below the xiphoid process and counted wrist flexion together with shoulder adduction and extension as positive.

Gerber 等在 1996 年描述了腹压试验，用于内旋受限、手放不到背后、做不了抬离试验的病人。按 2003 年一篇综述引用的原话：病人用手掌平按腹部，尽量让手臂保持最大内旋。内旋有力时，肘部不会向后落，保持在躯干前方。肩胛下肌无力时，保持不住最大内旋，病人感到无力，肘部向后落到躯干后方；病人改用伸肩而不是内旋来按压腹部（Gerber 1996，Tennent 2003 引用）。后来的作者补充了原话里没有的细节：Barth 2006 让手臂贴在体侧、屈肘 90°；Tokish 2003 让手掌从剑突正下方的上腹部开始，把屈腕加上肩内收和后伸算作阳性。

How accurate: Barth 2006 examined 68 consecutive patients before arthroscopy, which served as the reference standard; 20 had a subscapularis tear, partial or complete. The belly-press had a sensitivity of 40% and a specificity of 97.9%, and the authors linked a positive result to a tear of at least 30% of the tendon (Barth 2006). Barth counted the test positive if the patient was weaker than on the other side, or pushed the hand into the belly by extending the elbow or the shoulder, so these numbers are not for Gerber's elbow-drop sign alone. Other studies found different sensitivities: 27.8% for any subscapularis tear in 312 patients having arthroscopic cuff surgery (Yoon 2013, abstract), and 73.8%, with a specificity of 75.0%, for any subscapularis tear in the 120 patients in whom the belly-press was analysed, out of 144 having arthroscopic cuff repair (Nair 2025, Table 2). Across the three studies a negative test missed from about a quarter to nearly three quarters of the tears, so a negative belly-press does not rule a tear out.

有多准：Barth 2006 在 68 名连续的病人关节镜检查前做了检查，以关节镜为参考标准；其中 20 人有肩胛下肌撕裂（部分或完全）。腹压试验敏感度 40%、特异度 97.9%；作者认为阳性提示肌腱至少 30% 撕裂（Barth 2006）。Barth 的阳性标准是：与对侧相比无力，或靠伸肘、伸肩把手压向腹部；所以这组数字不是只按 Gerber 的“肘部后落”判读的。其他研究的敏感度不一样：在 312 名接受关节镜肩袖手术的病人中，对任何肩胛下肌撕裂的敏感度只有 27.8%（Yoon 2013 摘要）；在 144 名接受关节镜肩袖修补的病人中，有 120 人纳入腹压试验分析，对任何肩胛下肌撕裂的敏感度 73.8%、特异度 75.0%（Nair 2025 表 2）。三项研究里，检查阴性而漏掉的撕裂从约四分之一到将近四分之三不等，所以腹压试验阴性不能排除撕裂。

易混点：“抬离试验”这个名字指的东西不止一种：Gerber 1991 的抬离是病人主动把手背从背上抬起来；Gerber 1996 的“抬离”是检查者把手臂被动放到身后最大内旋的位置再松手，看病人能不能保持住，实际上是滞后征（Tennent 2003）。腹压试验常用于因为疼痛或活动受限做不了抬离试验的病人（Tokish 2003）。Tokish 2003 在 16 名健康志愿者身上测肌电，发现腹压试验更多激活肩胛下肌上部、抬离试验更多激活下部；Pennock 2011 在 20 名健康男性身上没有重复出这个差别。

Two patterns run through the studies quoted here. Neer and Hawkins-Kennedy are more sensitive than specific: a positive result adds little, and a negative one lowers the probability only somewhat. The lag signs and the belly-press are the other way round: specific but often insensitive, so a clear positive points to a tear, while a negative does not rule one out. No single test settles the question. The Cochrane review of 33 studies concluded that there is insufficient evidence on which to base the selection of physical tests for shoulder impingement (Hanchard 2013). That fits Tuesday's pathway, which, citing BESS 2025 and Hegedus 2012, recommended a combination of tests rather than any one test on its own.

这里引用的研究有两个规律。Neer 和 Hawkins-Kennedy 敏感度高于特异度：阳性帮助不大，阴性也只能让可能性降低一些。滞后征和腹压试验正好相反：特异但常常不敏感，明确的阳性提示有撕裂，阴性却不能排除。没有哪一项检查能单独下结论。Cochrane 系统综述纳入 33 项研究后认为，现有证据不足以据此选择肩撞击的体格检查（Hanchard 2013）。这与周二的流程一致：周二引用 BESS 2025 和 Hegedus 2012，主张把几项检查组合起来用，而不是只靠某一项。

Weakness: 75% accurate for the full can and 70% for the empty can. The full can provoked pain less often (43% against 50%), so the authors suggested it may be the more useful.

Calis 2000 found Hawkins 92.1% sensitive and 25.0% specific. Which result helps more, a negative or a positive one, and why?Calis 2000 中 Hawkins 敏感度 92.1%、特异度 25.0%。阴性和阳性哪个更有用？为什么？Show answer · 展开答案

A negative one, though only somewhat. With high sensitivity few people with the condition test negative, so a negative result lowers the probability of subacromial pain; with low specificity many people without it test positive, so a positive adds little. In the same study about half of the shoulders with a negative Hawkins test (44%) still had a positive injection test, so a negative result does not rule it out.

阴性更有用，但也只是有限地有用。敏感度高，有病的人很少呈阴性，所以阴性让肩峰下疼痛的可能性降低；特异度低，很多没病的人也呈阳性，所以阳性帮助不大。同一项研究里，Hawkins 阴性的肩仍有约 44% 注射试验阳性，所以阴性不能排除。

Set up the external rotation lag sign: where is the arm, what does the examiner let go of, and what is a positive result?外旋滞后征怎么摆：手臂放在哪里？检查者松开什么？怎样算阳性？Show answer · 展开答案

Shoulder raised 20° in the scapular plane, elbow at 90°, turned out to 5° short of full external rotation. The examiner lets go of the wrist but keeps supporting the elbow. Positive: the forearm drifts back inward; the angle lost is the lag.

肩在肩胛平面抬高 20°，屈肘 90°，外旋到比最大外旋少 5°。检查者松开手腕，但继续托住肘部。阳性：前臂向内回漂，丢掉的角度就是滞后。

The hornblower sign was 100% sensitive in Walch 1998 but 17% in the ROW cohort. Why can both be true?Hornblower 征在 Walch 1998 敏感度 100%，在 ROW 队列只有 17%。为什么两者可以同时成立？Show answer · 展开答案

They asked different questions in different patients. Walch looked for stage 3–4 fatty degeneration of teres minor in 54 people operated on for combined supraspinatus and infraspinatus tears; ROW looked for any infraspinatus tear in patients over 45 with shoulder pain.

两项研究在不同病人身上问的是不同的问题。Walch 是在 54 名因冈上肌和冈下肌联合撕裂手术的病人中找小圆肌 3–4 期脂肪变性；ROW 是在 45 岁以上肩痛病人中找任何冈下肌撕裂。

Describe a positive belly-press test in Gerber's words, and say what Barth 2006 found for its sensitivity and specificity.按 Gerber 的原话描述腹压试验阳性，并说出 Barth 2006 的敏感度和特异度。Show answer · 展开答案

Maximal internal rotation cannot be held, the patient feels weak, and the elbow drops back behind the trunk; the patient presses by extending the shoulder instead of rotating it inward. Barth: sensitivity 40%, specificity 97.9%, with arthroscopy as the reference.

Take-home · 本章要点
Each cuff test has a position, a positive criterion and, in each study, a target condition. Read the empty and full can for weakness; treat a positive Neer or Hawkins-Kennedy as weak evidence, and a negative one as lowering the probability only somewhat; treat a clear lag sign, hornblower sign or belly-press as strong evidence of a tear. In general painful shoulders a negative one does not rule a tear out, although in patients already known to have combined or massive tears a negative hornblower sign (Walch 1998) or lag sign (Collin 2015) made severe teres minor degeneration unlikely. Quote every number with its study and target.每项肩袖检查都有体位、阳性标准，以及每项研究各自的目标疾病。空罐、满罐按无力判读；Neer 或 Hawkins-Kennedy 阳性只是弱证据，阴性也只能让可能性降低一些；滞后征、Hornblower 征或腹压试验明确阳性是撕裂的有力证据。在一般肩痛病人中，阴性不能排除撕裂；但在已知有联合撕裂或巨大撕裂的病人中，Hornblower 征阴性（Walch 1998）或滞后征阴性（Collin 2015）提示小圆肌严重变性的可能性很小。引用每个数字都要带上研究和目标疾病。
