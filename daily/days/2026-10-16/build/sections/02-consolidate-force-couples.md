### Two force couples｜两组力偶

At the glenohumeral joint, the deltoid and rotator cuff work together to elevate the arm while controlling the position of the humeral head. At the scapula, the upper and lower trapezius work with serratus anterior to produce upward rotation during arm elevation.

在盂肱关节，三角肌与肩袖协同抬臂，并控制肱骨头的位置。在肩胛骨，上、下斜方肌与前锯肌协作，在上举过程中产生肩胛骨上旋。

![两组力偶，按章节图 10、11 重画，拉力、方向、神经和旋转轴与原图一致（省略了起止点对照栏）。左：三角肌把肱骨往上拉；冈上肌把肱骨头压向关节盂；冈下肌、小圆肌和肩胛下肌把它往下、往内拉。右：三个拉力让肩胛骨上旋；章节图 11 把早期旋转轴标在肩胛冈根部附近，后期旋转轴在肩锁关节。](资源/06-两组力偶.svg)

*The two couples, redrawn from the chapter's figures 10 and 11 with the same pulls, directions, nerves and axes (the origin–insertion panels are left out). Left: the deltoid pulls the humerus up; supraspinatus presses the head toward the glenoid; infraspinatus, teres minor and subscapularis pull it down and in. Right: three pulls on the scapula turn it into upward rotation; the chapter's figure 11 marks the early axis near the root of the spine, and the late axis is at the acromioclavicular joint.*  
*两组力偶，按章节图 10、11 重画，拉力、方向、神经和旋转轴与原图一致（省略了起止点对照栏）。左：三角肌把肱骨往上拉；冈上肌把肱骨头压向关节盂；冈下肌、小圆肌和肩胛下肌把它往下、往内拉。右：三个拉力让肩胛骨上旋；章节图 11 把早期旋转轴标在肩胛冈根部附近，后期旋转轴在肩锁关节。*

**Say it aloud from memory｜口述**：Shoulder joint: how do the deltoid and the rotator cuff work together as the arm rises? Say what each one pulls and in which direction, and what would happen if the deltoid acted alone.<br>盂肱关节：手臂上举时三角肌和肩袖怎样配合？说出各自拉什么、朝哪个方向，以及三角肌单独作用会怎样。

<details><summary>Reference 原文</summary>

The deltoid pulls the humerus upward; acting alone, it would move the humeral head up toward the acromion. Supraspinatus compresses the head toward the glenoid, and infraspinatus, teres minor and subscapularis pull the head inferomedially, down and in. Together they elevate the arm while controlling the position of the humeral head, which stays centred beneath the acromion.<br>三角肌把肱骨往上拉；如果单独作用，会让肱骨头向肩峰方向上移。冈上肌把肱骨头压向关节盂，冈下肌、小圆肌和肩胛下肌把肱骨头往下、往内拉。合起来，它们在抬臂的同时控制肱骨头的位置，让它保持在肩峰下方的中间。

</details>

**Say it aloud from memory｜口述**：Scapula: name the three parts of the upward-rotation couple. For each, say where it pulls, in which direction, and which nerve supplies it. Then say where the axis of rotation lies early and late in elevation.<br>肩胛骨：说出上旋力偶的三个部分，每个拉在哪里、朝哪个方向、由哪条神经支配；再说旋转轴在上举早期和后期分别在哪里。

<details><summary>Reference 原文</summary>

Upper trapezius, supplied by the accessory nerve: a superomedial pull on the acromion and lateral clavicle. Lower trapezius, also the accessory nerve: an inferomedial pull on the root of the scapular spine. Lower serratus anterior, supplied by the long thoracic nerve: a lateral and anterior pull on the inferior angle. Together they produce upward rotation of the scapula during arm elevation. In the chapter's figure 11 the early axis is marked near the root of the spine; the late axis is at the acromioclavicular joint.<br>上斜方肌，副神经支配：把肩峰和锁骨外端往上、往内拉。下斜方肌，同样是副神经：把肩胛冈根部往下、往内拉。前锯肌下部，胸长神经支配：把肩胛下角往外、往前拉。三者合起来，在手臂上举时让肩胛骨上旋。章节图 11 把早期旋转轴标在肩胛冈根部附近，后期旋转轴在肩锁关节。

</details>

### Trace the muscles from memory｜凭记忆讲出肌肉走行

Each answer is the chapter's course sentence, then its nerve and the action that matters here. Say all of it: origin, path, insertion, nerve.

每个参考答案都是章节里的走行句，再加神经和这里用到的动作。要完整说出：起点、走向、止点、神经。

**Say it aloud from memory｜口述**：Trace the deltoid: origin, path, insertion, nerve with its segments, and which part contributes strongly to abduction.<br>讲出三角肌：起点、走向、止点、神经和节段，以及哪一部分是外展的重要参与者。

<details><summary>Reference 原文</summary>

The deltoid originates from the lateral third of the clavicle, the acromion, and the spine of the scapula, passes toward the humerus as its fibers converge, and inserts onto the deltoid tuberosity of the humerus. It is supplied by the axillary nerve, mainly C5–C6. The middle, or acromial, part contributes strongly to arm abduction.<br>三角肌起自锁骨外侧三分之一、肩峰和肩胛冈，肌纤维向肱骨汇聚走行，止于肱骨三角肌粗隆。由腋神经支配，主要为 C5–C6。其中部，即肩峰部，是手臂外展的重要参与者。

</details>

**Say it aloud from memory｜口述**：Trace the lower trapezius: origin with its vertebral levels, direction of travel, insertion, nerve, and its role in the scapular couple.<br>讲出下斜方肌：起点和椎体节段、走行方向、止点、神经，以及它在肩胛骨力偶中的作用。

<details><summary>Reference 原文</summary>

The lower trapezius originates from the spinous processes of the lower thoracic vertebrae, commonly described as T4–T12, passes superolaterally, and inserts onto the scapular spine near its medial end. It is supplied by the accessory nerve (cranial nerve XI); the anterior rami of C3–C4 are usually described as proprioceptive. Its fibers work with the upper trapezius and serratus anterior to produce upward rotation of the scapula during arm elevation.<br>下斜方肌起自下部胸椎棘突（常描述为 T4–T12），向上外侧走行，止于肩胛冈内侧端附近。由副神经（第十一对脑神经）支配；C3–C4 脊神经前支通常被描述为本体感觉纤维。它与上斜方肌和前锯肌协作，在手臂上举时产生肩胛骨上旋。

</details>

**Say it aloud from memory｜口述**：Trace serratus anterior: origin, how it reaches the scapula, insertion, nerve with its segments, and which fibres help upward rotation.<br>讲出前锯肌：起点、怎样到达肩胛骨、止点、神经和节段，以及哪部分纤维参与上旋。

<details><summary>Reference 原文</summary>

The serratus anterior originates from the lateral surfaces of the upper eight or nine ribs, passes around the thoracic wall, and inserts onto the anterior, or costal, surface of the medial border of the scapula. It is supplied by the long thoracic nerve, C5–C7. Its inferior fibres attach near the inferior angle and contribute to upward rotation.<br>前锯肌起自上八或九根肋骨的外侧面，绕过胸壁，止于肩胛骨内侧缘的前面（肋面）。由胸长神经支配，C5–C7。其下部纤维附着于下角附近，参与肩胛骨上旋。

</details>

### Scapulohumeral rhythm｜肩肱节律

Arm elevation combines glenohumeral motion with scapulothoracic motion. The familiar 2:1 ratio is a teaching approximation, not a fixed ratio at every angle. Scapular motion varies with the task, the plane of elevation, and the individual.

手臂上举结合了盂肱关节活动和肩胛胸壁活动。常见的 2∶1 是教学概括，并不是每个角度都恒定不变的比例。肩胛骨运动会随任务、上举平面与个体而变化。

![章节图 12，原样使用。30° 时以盂肱运动为主（定位期）；90° 时约为盂肱 60° 加肩胛 30°，180° 时约为 120° 加 60°。章节对肩肱节律引用的是 Inman（1944）和 Bagg 与 Forrest（1988）。](资源/07-肩肱节律.svg)

*The chapter's figure 12, unchanged. At 30° the motion is mostly glenohumeral (the setting phase); at 90° it is about 60° glenohumeral plus 30° scapular, and at 180° about 120° plus 60°. The chapter cites Inman (1944) and Bagg and Forrest (1988) for the rhythm.*  
*章节图 12，原样使用。30° 时以盂肱运动为主（定位期）；90° 时约为盂肱 60° 加肩胛 30°，180° 时约为 120° 加 60°。章节对肩肱节律引用的是 Inman（1944）和 Bagg 与 Forrest（1988）。*

**Say it aloud from memory｜口述**：Describe elevation from 0° to 180° in three overlapping stages. For each, say what the glenohumeral joint and the scapula are doing and which muscles contribute.<br>把 0° 到 180° 的上举分成三个相互重叠的阶段来讲：每个阶段盂肱关节和肩胛骨在做什么，哪些肌肉参与。

<details><summary>Reference 原文</summary>

0–30°, initiation and positioning: supraspinatus and deltoid contribute to initiation of elevation; the rotator cuff helps stabilise the humeral head while the scapular muscles position the scapula. 30–90°, coordinated upward rotation: glenohumeral and scapular motion occur together; trapezius and serratus anterior contribute to scapular upward rotation, and the clavicle also moves. 90–180°: continued scapular upward rotation and posterior tilt, together with humeral external rotation and clavicular motion. These are overlapping contributions rather than isolated on-off stages.<br>0–30°，起动与定位：冈上肌与三角肌参与上举起动；肩袖帮助稳定肱骨头，肩胛肌群调节肩胛骨位置。30–90°，协同上旋：盂肱关节与肩胛骨活动相互配合；斜方肌与前锯肌参与肩胛骨上旋，锁骨也同时运动。90–180°：肩胛骨持续上旋、后倾，以及肱骨外旋和锁骨运动。这些作用相互重叠，并不是彼此孤立、依次开关的阶段。

</details>

**Say it aloud from memory｜口述**：A classmate says the shoulder always moves 2° at the glenohumeral joint for every 1° of the scapula. Answer them, using the numbers in figure 07.<br>同学说肩关节每个角度都是盂肱 2° 配肩胛 1°。用图 07 的数字回答他。

<details><summary>Reference 原文</summary>

2:1 is a teaching approximation, not a fixed ratio at every angle; scapular motion varies with the task, the plane of elevation and the individual. In figure 07 the first 30° is mostly glenohumeral, the setting phase. The about-2:1 split describes the totals at 90° (60° plus 30°) and at 180° (120° plus 60°).<br>2∶1 是教学概括，不是每个角度都恒定的比例；肩胛骨运动随任务、上举平面和个体而变化。图 07 里前 30° 以盂肱运动为主，是定位期。约 2∶1 指的是 90°（60° 加 30°）和 180°（120° 加 60°）时的总量。

</details>

### When one partner is weak｜力偶中一方无力时

**Say it aloud from memory｜口述**：Predict what you would see in each case: (a) serratus anterior is weak; (b) the upper trapezius is overactive and the lower trapezius is weak; (c) the rotator cuff is weak.<br>推断每种情况会看到什么：(a) 前锯肌无力；(b) 上斜方肌过度活跃、下斜方肌无力；(c) 肩袖无力。

<details><summary>Reference 原文</summary>

(a) Insufficient scapular upward rotation, scapular winging and difficulty elevating the arm. (b) The person compensates by shrugging the shoulder. (c) Rotator cuff weakness involves the deltoid–rotator cuff force couple, upward movement of the humeral head, the subacromial space and pain.<br>(a) 肩胛上旋不足、翼状肩胛和上举困难。(b) 出现耸肩代偿。(c) 肩袖无力涉及三角肌—肩袖力偶、肱骨头上移、肩峰下间隙与疼痛。

</details>

#### Check yourself｜先自测

**Q.** Which muscle in the glenohumeral couple would, acting alone, move the humeral head up toward the acromion?<br>盂肱关节力偶中，哪块肌肉单独作用会让肱骨头向肩峰上移？

<details><summary>Answer 答案</summary>

The deltoid. The rotator cuff counters it: supraspinatus compresses the head and the other three pull it down and in.<br>三角肌。肩袖与它对抗：冈上肌压住肱骨头，另外三块把它往下、往内拉。

</details>

**Q.** Which two parts of the scapular couple share a nerve, and what is it?<br>肩胛骨力偶里哪两部分是同一条神经支配？是哪条？

<details><summary>Answer 答案</summary>

Upper and lower trapezius, both supplied by the accessory nerve (cranial nerve XI).<br>上斜方肌和下斜方肌，都由副神经（第十一对脑神经）支配。

</details>

**Q.** Where does the lower serratus anterior pull, and in which direction?<br>前锯肌下部拉在哪里，朝哪个方向？

<details><summary>Answer 答案</summary>

On the inferior angle of the scapula, laterally and anteriorly.<br>拉在肩胛下角，方向向外、向前。

</details>

**Q.** In figure 07, how much of the 90° is glenohumeral and how much is scapular?<br>图 07 中，90° 里盂肱和肩胛各占多少？

<details><summary>Answer 答案</summary>

About 60° glenohumeral and 30° scapular, which is about 2:1.<br>约盂肱 60°、肩胛 30°，约为 2∶1。

</details>

> **Take-home 本章要点：** Two couples raise the arm together. At the glenohumeral joint the deltoid lifts the humerus while the cuff keeps the head centred; at the scapula the upper and lower trapezius (accessory nerve) and lower serratus anterior (long thoracic nerve) turn it into upward rotation. Elevation combines glenohumeral and scapulothoracic motion in overlapping stages, and 2:1 is an approximation, not a rule.  
> 两组力偶一起把手臂抬起来。在盂肱关节，三角肌把肱骨往上抬，肩袖让肱骨头保持居中；在肩胛骨，上、下斜方肌（副神经）和前锯肌下部（胸长神经）让它上旋。上举结合了盂肱关节活动和肩胛胸壁活动，按相互重叠的阶段完成；2∶1 是近似，不是定律。
