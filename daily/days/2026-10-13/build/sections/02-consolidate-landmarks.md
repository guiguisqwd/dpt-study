**Q.** The suprascapular nerve can be compressed at F or at E. Which muscles are affected in each case?<br>肩胛上神经可能在 F 或 E 处受压。两种情况分别累及哪些肌肉？

<details><summary>Answer 答案</summary>

F, the suprascapular notch: both supraspinatus and infraspinatus. E, the spinoglenoid notch: infraspinatus only; supraspinatus is spared.<br>F 肩胛上切迹：冈上肌和冈下肌都受累。E 冈盂切迹：只有冈下肌，冈上肌不受累。

</details>

**Q.** Which landmark does the axillary nerve wind around, and which space does that landmark bound?<br>腋神经绕行哪个骨性标志？这个标志是哪个间隙的边界？

<details><summary>Answer 答案</summary>

The surgical neck of the humerus; it is the lateral boundary of the quadrangular space.<br>肱骨外科颈；它是四边孔的外界。

</details>

**Q.** Teres minor and teres major: which part of the scapula does each originate from?<br>小圆肌和大圆肌各起于肩胛骨的哪个部位？

<details><summary>Answer 答案</summary>

Teres minor: the upper part of the lateral border. Teres major: the posterior surface of the inferior angle.<br>小圆肌：外侧缘上部。大圆肌：下角后面。

</details>

**Q.** Fill in: The deltoid originates from the lateral third of the clavicle, the ____ and the ____, and inserts onto the ____ of the humerus.<br>填空：三角肌起于锁骨外侧三分之一、____和____，止于肱骨____。

<details><summary>Answer 答案</summary>

Acromion; spine of the scapula; deltoid tuberosity.<br>肩峰；肩胛冈；三角肌粗隆。

</details>

**Q.** Which landmark separates the greater and lesser tubercles, and which muscle inserts onto its medial lip?<br>哪个骨性标志分隔大结节和小结节？哪块肌肉止于它的内侧唇？

<details><summary>Answer 答案</summary>

The intertubercular groove; the teres major (medial lip, the crest of the lesser tubercle).<br>结节间沟；大圆肌（内侧唇，即小结节嵴）。

</details>

**Q.** Which tendon passes beneath the acromion, and on which facet does it insert?<br>哪条肌腱从肩峰下方经过？止于哪个小面？

<details><summary>Answer 答案</summary>

The supraspinatus tendon; the superior facet of the greater tubercle.<br>冈上肌腱；大结节上小面。

</details>

### Key: what each letter marks｜答案对照：每个字母指什么

| Letter 字母 | Landmark 骨性标志 | What attaches or passes here 附着或经过的结构 |
|---|---|---|
| A | Acromion 肩峰 | The lateral extension of the scapular spine; it arches over the glenohumeral joint and articulates with the clavicle. The supraspinatus tendon passes beneath it, and part of the deltoid originates from it.<br>肩胛冈向外侧的延伸，呈弓形覆盖在盂肱关节上方，并与锁骨相关节。冈上肌腱从它下方经过，三角肌也有一部分起于肩峰。 |
| B | Scapular spine 肩胛冈 | The horizontal bony ridge you follow from the acromion to the medial border. Part of the deltoid originates from it; the trapezius inserts onto its superior lip as far as the tubercle at its medial end, and the lower trapezius near that medial end.<br>从肩峰往内侧一直摸到肩胛骨内侧缘的那条横骨嵴。三角肌有一部分起于肩胛冈；斜方肌止于肩胛冈上唇直至其内侧端的结节，下斜方肌止于内侧端附近。 |
| C | Supraspinous fossa 冈上窝 | The smaller area on the posterior surface, above the spine. The supraspinatus originates here; the suprascapular nerve crosses it and gives motor branches to that muscle.<br>肩胛骨后面、肩胛冈上方较小的区域。冈上肌起于此处；肩胛上神经横过冈上窝，并向冈上肌发出运动支。 |
| D | Infraspinous fossa 冈下窝 | The larger area on the posterior surface, below the spine. The infraspinatus originates here; the suprascapular nerve reaches it through the spinoglenoid notch.<br>肩胛骨后面、肩胛冈下方较大的区域。冈下肌起于此处；肩胛上神经经冈盂切迹进入冈下窝。 |
| E | Spinoglenoid notch 冈盂切迹 | Behind the scapular neck and beneath the acromion; it connects the two fossae. The suprascapular nerve curves around the lateral border of the spine through it, so compression here can cause isolated atrophy of the infraspinatus while the supraspinatus is spared.<br>在肩胛颈后方、肩峰下方，连通冈上窝和冈下窝。肩胛上神经绕过肩胛冈外侧缘经此切迹进入冈下窝，所以这里受压可只引起冈下肌萎缩，冈上肌不受累。 |
| F | Suprascapular notch 肩胛上切迹 | Where the superior border meets the base of the coracoid process, just medial to it. The suprascapular nerve passes through it beneath the superior transverse scapular ligament, so compression here can affect both supraspinatus and infraspinatus.<br>肩胛骨上缘与喙突基底部相接处，在喙突内侧。肩胛上神经从肩胛上横韧带下方穿过此切迹，所以这里受压可同时累及冈上肌和冈下肌。 |
| G | Medial border of the scapula 肩胛骨内侧缘 | The serratus anterior inserts onto its anterior, or costal, surface.<br>前锯肌止于内侧缘的前面（肋面）。 |
| H | Lateral border of the scapula 肩胛骨外侧缘 | The teres minor originates from its upper part.<br>小圆肌起于外侧缘上部。 |
| I | Inferior angle 下角 | The teres major originates from its posterior surface.<br>大圆肌起于下角后面。 |
| J | Greater tubercle 大结节 | The bony prominence on the lateral side of the proximal humerus. The supraspinatus, infraspinatus and teres minor insert onto its superior, middle and inferior facets.<br>肱骨近端外侧的骨性隆起。冈上肌、冈下肌和小圆肌分别止于它的上、中、下小面。 |
| K | Surgical neck 外科颈 | The axillary nerve winds around it deep to the deltoid after passing through the quadrangular space; the surgical neck is the lateral boundary of that space.<br>腋神经穿过四边孔后，在三角肌深面绕行外科颈；外科颈是四边孔的外界。 |
| L | Deltoid tuberosity 三角肌粗隆 | The deltoid inserts here.<br>三角肌止于此处。 |
| M | Coracoid process 喙突 | The suprascapular notch lies just medial to it, and the glenoid cavity lies inferior to it.<br>肩胛上切迹在它内侧，关节盂在它下方。 |
| N | Glenoid cavity 关节盂 | The shallow socket at the upper end of the lateral border, where the head of the humerus articulates to form the glenohumeral joint; the rotator cuff helps hold the humeral head in it.<br>肩胛骨外侧缘上端的浅窝，与肱骨头相关节构成盂肱关节；肩袖帮助把肱骨头稳定在关节盂内。 |
| O | Subscapular fossa 肩胛下窝 | The large concave depression covering most of the costal (anterior) surface. The subscapularis originates from it.<br>肩胛骨肋面（前面）上的大片凹陷。肩胛下肌起于肩胛下窝。 |
| P | Lesser tubercle 小结节 | The smaller prominence on the anterior surface of the proximal humerus, medial to the greater tubercle across the intertubercular groove. The subscapularis inserts onto it.<br>肱骨近端前面较小的隆起，隔着结节间沟位于大结节内侧。肩胛下肌止于小结节。 |
| Q | Intertubercular groove 结节间沟 | It separates the greater and lesser tubercles. The teres major inserts onto its medial lip (the crest of the lesser tubercle).<br>分隔大结节和小结节。大圆肌止于结节间沟内侧唇（小结节嵴）。 |

Palpation from the chapter: from the acromion, palpate medially until you feel a horizontal bony ridge, the scapular spine, and follow it all the way to the medial border of the scapula.

章节里的触诊：从肩峰往内侧摸，摸到一条横的骨嵴，就是肩胛冈，一直摸到肩胛骨内侧缘。

> **Take-home 本章要点：** Three fossae (supraspinous, infraspinous, subscapular) for three cuff origins; two tubercles for the four insertions; two notches decide which muscles a suprascapular nerve lesion weakens.  
> 三个窝（冈上、冈下、肩胛下）是三块肩袖肌的起点；两个结节是四块肩袖肌的止点；两个切迹决定肩胛上神经受压时哪些肌肉无力。
