Key terms 关键词（点按钮听发音）

Subacromial pain syndrome 肩峰下疼痛综合征sub-uh-kroh-mee-uhl payn sin-drohm /ˌsʌbəˈkroʊmiəl peɪn ˈsɪnˌdroʊm/Subacromial space 肩峰下间隙sub-uh-kroh-mee-uhl spays /ˌsʌbəˈkroʊmiəl speɪs/Subacromial bursa 肩峰下滑囊sub-uh-kroh-mee-uhl bur-suh /ˌsʌbəˈkroʊmiəl ˈbɝsə/Subacromial impingement 肩峰下撞击sub-uh-kroh-mee-uhl im-pinj-muhnt /ˌsʌbəˈkroʊmiəl ɪmˈpɪndʒmənt/Rotator cuff tendinopathy 肩袖肌腱病roh-tay-ter kuf ten-duh-nah-puh-thee /ˈroʊˌteɪtər kʌf ˌtɛndəˈnɑpəθi/Painful arc 疼痛弧payn-fuhl ahrk /ˈpeɪnfəl ɑrk/Palpation 触诊pal-pay-shuhn /pælˈpeɪʃən/Adhesive capsulitis 粘连性关节囊炎ad-hee-siv kap-suh-lye-tuhs /ædˈhisɪv ˌkæpsəˈlaɪtəs/Glenohumeral osteoarthritis 盂肱关节骨关节炎glen-oh-hyoo-muh-ruhl ah-stee-oh-ahr-thry-tis /ˌɡlɛnoʊˈhjumərəl ˌɑstioʊɑrˈθraɪtɪs/Cervical radiculopathy 颈神经根病sur-vih-kuhl ruh-dik-yuh-lah-puh-thee /ˈsɝvɪkəl rəˌdɪkjəˈlɑpəθi/Corticosteroid injection 皮质类固醇注射kor-tuh-koh-ster-oyd in-jek-shuhn /ˌkɔrtəkoʊˈstɛrɔɪd ɪnˈdʒɛkʃən/Subacromial decompression 肩峰下减压术sub-uh-kroh-mee-uhl dee-kuhm-presh-uhn /ˌsʌbəˈkroʊmiəl ˌdikəmˈprɛʃən/Dry needling 干针dry need-ling /draɪ ˈnidlɪŋ/

Subacromial pain syndrome (SAPS) is the Dutch guideline's name for non-traumatic, usually one-sided shoulder pain located around the acromion that often gets worse during or after lifting the arm (Diercks 2014). It is an umbrella diagnosis: bursitis, calcific tendinosis, supraspinatus tendinopathy, partial cuff tears, biceps tendinitis and cuff degeneration all sit under it. Lewis (2016) uses a wider umbrella, rotator cuff related shoulder pain (RCRSP), which also covers symptomatic full-thickness tears. You will meet both names, and the older word impingement, in papers and referral letters.

肩峰下疼痛综合征（SAPS）是荷兰指南的叫法：非外伤性、通常单侧、位于肩峰周围的肩痛，抬臂时或抬臂后常加重（Diercks 2014）。它是一个统称：滑囊炎、钙化性肌腱病、冈上肌腱病、肩袖部分撕裂、肱二头肌腱炎和肩袖退变都归在里面。Lewis（2016）用的统称更宽，叫肩袖相关肩痛（RCRSP），还包括有症状的全层撕裂。论文和转诊单里这两个名字都会见到，还有旧称“撞击”（impingement）。

The space is typically 1 to 1.5 cm high and narrows normally during abduction and forward flexion; superior migration of the humeral head, or too little scapular upward rotation and posterior tilt, narrows it further (StatPearls). The supraspinatus tendon runs under the acromion to the superior facet of the greater tubercle, and the subacromial bursa separates it from the coracoacromial ligament, the acromion and the deltoid (Kenhub). The part of the bursa that extends laterally deep to the deltoid is usually called the subdeltoid bursa.

这个间隙通常高 1–1.5 cm，外展和前屈时本来就会变窄；肱骨头上移，或肩胛骨上回旋和后倾不够，会让它更窄（StatPearls）。冈上肌腱从肩峰下方走到大结节上小面，肩峰下滑囊把它和喙肩韧带、肩峰、三角肌隔开（Kenhub）。滑囊向外延伸到三角肌深面的部分，通常叫三角肌下滑囊。

The pathway in six steps六步流程

  1. History: what the patient tells you 主诉与问诊

    The typical story is pain around the upper arm that came on gradually, is made worse by shoulder-level or overhead activities, and followed no trauma (BESS 2025). The deltoid region is the area patients report most often (Requejo-Salinas 2022), and pain from the rotator cuff usually spreads no farther than the elbow (AAFP 2008). Ask about sleep and night pain, how easily the pain flares and how long it lasts once provoked; Lewis (2016) calls a shoulder that flares easily, stays painful for a long time and hurts at night irritable.

    典型主诉是：上臂周围的疼痛逐渐出现，做到与肩同高或过头的动作时加重，之前没有外伤（BESS 2025）。病人最常指出的疼痛部位是三角肌区（Requejo-Salinas 2022）；肩袖来源的疼痛通常不超过肘部（AAFP 2008）。要问睡眠和夜间痛，问疼痛多容易被诱发、诱发后持续多久；Lewis（2016）把容易诱发、持续时间长、夜里也痛的情况称为“易激惹型”。

  2. Observation and palpation 观察与触诊

    Watch both arms rise together. The Dutch guideline says that judging range of motion by eye is useful only for comparing the painful side with the other side (Diercks 2014). Palpate around the acromion: subacromial tenderness may suggest rotator cuff pathology (AAFP 2008), while pain localised to the acromioclavicular joint with tenderness there points to that joint (BESS/BOA 2014). The opened guidelines give no other palpation findings for subacromial pain.

    看双臂一起抬起。荷兰指南指出，目测关节活动度只适合拿患侧和健侧比较（Diercks 2014）。在肩峰周围触诊：肩峰下压痛可能提示肩袖病变（AAFP 2008）；疼痛局限在肩锁关节并有压痛，则指向肩锁关节（BESS/BOA 2014）。查到的指南没有给出肩峰下疼痛的其他触诊表现。

  3. Range of motion, strength and special tests 关节活动度、肌力与特殊检查

    Compare active with passive range. In rotator cuff pathology, active range is often lost while passive range is relatively preserved (AAFP 2008), and subacromial pain usually comes with full passive range of the glenohumeral joint (Kulkarni 2015). Test the painful arc (figure 02) and resisted strength against the other side. BESS (2025) calls subacromial pain syndrome a clinical diagnosis and says a combination of the Hawkins-Kennedy, Neer, Jobe and external rotation resistance tests is useful; a 2012 meta-analysis concluded that no single test can be recommended on its own to make the diagnosis (Hegedus 2012). Friday's chapter covers how each test is done.

    先比较主动和被动活动度。肩袖病变常见主动活动度减少、被动活动度相对保留（AAFP 2008）；肩峰下疼痛通常伴有盂肱关节被动活动度完整（Kulkarni 2015）。检查疼痛弧（图 02），并和健侧比较抗阻肌力。BESS（2025）说肩峰下疼痛综合征是临床诊断，Hawkins-Kennedy、Neer、Jobe 和外旋抗阻几项检查组合起来有用；2012 年的荟萃分析认为，没有哪一项检查能单独用来下诊断（Hegedus 2012）。各项检查怎么做，放在周五那一章。

  4. Differential diagnosis and referral signals 鉴别诊断与转诊信号

    The diagnosis assumes no trauma and no sign of a cuff tear, arthritis, frozen shoulder, instability, or neck or nerve problems (BESS 2025). A large loss of passive external rotation suggests frozen shoulder or glenohumeral osteoarthritis; neck movements that bring on the arm symptoms suggest the cervical spine; a sudden loss of active elevation suggests an acute cuff tear. A hot, red joint and an unreduced dislocation need same-day referral (BESS/BOA 2014). The table below lists each sign with its source.

    这个诊断的前提是：没有外伤，也没有肩袖撕裂、关节炎、冻结肩、不稳或颈椎与神经问题的表现（BESS 2025）。被动外旋明显受限，要想到冻结肩或盂肱关节骨关节炎；颈部动作引出上肢症状，要想到颈椎；突然不能主动抬臂，要想到急性肩袖撕裂。关节红热和未复位的脱位要当天转诊（BESS/BOA 2014）。下面的表逐条列出信号和来源。

  5. Treatment: education, exercise, manual therapy, needling 治疗：宣教、运动处方、手法、针刺或干针

    Start with advice: relative rest in the acute phase, then a gradual return to activity (Diercks 2014), moving within the patient's comfort zone (Lambers Heerspink 2026). Exercise is the core: low intensity, high frequency, within the pain threshold and focused on eccentric training (Diercks 2014), aimed at the rotator cuff and the scapular stabilisers rather than general exercise. Adding joint mobilisation to exercise gives little or no extra benefit (Diercks 2014; Steuri 2017). The Dutch guideline found acupuncture no more effective than placebo and gave no recommendation on dry needling. Injection and surgery are medical options; the notes below give the evidence.

    先做宣教：急性期相对休息，然后逐步恢复活动（Diercks 2014），在病人能接受的范围内活动（Lambers Heerspink 2026）。运动是核心：低强度、高频率、不超过疼痛阈值、侧重离心训练（Diercks 2014），练肩袖和肩胛稳定肌，而不是一般性锻炼。在运动之外加关节松动，额外好处很小或没有（Diercks 2014；Steuri 2017）。荷兰指南认为针刺不比安慰治疗更有效，对干针没有给推荐。注射和手术属于医疗选项，证据见下文细讲。

  6. Progression and return to activity 进阶与回归标准

    Let the symptoms guide progression: change one thing at a time and watch its effect over 24 hours; as pain settles, both range and resistance can be increased (Lewis 2016). In the Holmgren trial, the load went up every other week with weights and elastic bands over 12 weeks (Holmgren 2012). BESS (2025) refers patients who have not improved after 12 weeks of non-operative care to secondary care. The opened sources give no numeric return-to-sport criteria; for work, they advise planning the return early, together with the worker (Lafrance 2022).

    按症状进阶：一次只改一个变量，看它在 24 小时内的影响；疼痛减轻后，活动范围和阻力都可以加（Lewis 2016）。Holmgren 试验在 12 周里每两周用哑铃和弹力带加一次负荷（Holmgren 2012）。BESS（2025）规定，非手术治疗 12 周仍无改善的病人转专科。查到的来源没有给出重返运动的量化标准；重返工作方面，只建议尽早和病人一起制定计划（Lafrance 2022）。

Steps 1 to 3 in more detail第 1–3 步细讲

Age and night pain are weaker clues than they look. AAFP (2008) describes patients with rotator cuff pathology as typically over 40, but in an international Delphi study of physical therapists the descriptor 'age typically over 40' did not reach consensus, and the authors note that cuff disorders appear across a wide age range (Requejo-Salinas 2022). Night pain from lying on the affected shoulder has been associated with cuff tears, and AAFP also lists night pain under cuff disorders and adhesive capsulitis; the Delphi experts agreed only that this condition affects sleep. So ask every patient about sleep, but do not use night pain to pick the diagnosis.

年龄和夜间痛作为线索，比看上去弱。AAFP（2008）说肩袖病变的病人通常在 40 岁以上；但在一项国际物理治疗师 Delphi 研究里，“年龄通常超过 40 岁”这一条没有达成共识，作者指出肩袖疾病在很宽的年龄范围内都会出现（Requejo-Salinas 2022）。压着患肩睡觉时的夜间痛与肩袖撕裂有关，AAFP 也把夜间痛列在肩袖疾病和粘连性关节囊炎下面；Delphi 专家只同意“这种肩痛影响睡眠”。所以每个病人都要问睡眠，但不要靠夜间痛来定诊断。

The painful arc has no single agreed range. AAFP (2008) defines it as pain with active abduction between 60 and 100 degrees; StatPearls gives about 70 to 120 degrees, with relative relief above that; TeachMeAnatomy gives 60 to 120 degrees. In a JAMA review of referred patients examined by specialists, a positive painful arc had a positive likelihood ratio of 3.7 (95% CI 1.9–7.0) for rotator cuff disease, and a normal painful arc had a negative likelihood ratio of 0.36 (0.23–0.54); whether this holds for patients seen first in primary care is unknown (Hermans 2013). Weakness alone does not separate tendinopathy from a tear: on the empty can test, weakness compared with the other side may indicate rotator cuff pathology, including supraspinatus tendinopathy or tear (AAFP 2008).

疼痛弧没有统一的范围。AAFP（2008）定义为主动外展 60–100° 之间出现疼痛；StatPearls 写约 70–120°，超过后相对缓解；TeachMeAnatomy 写 60–120°。JAMA 的一篇综述纳入的是转诊来、由专科医生检查的病人：疼痛弧阳性对肩袖疾病的阳性似然比为 3.7（95% CI 1.9–7.0），疼痛弧正常的阴性似然比为 0.36（0.23–0.54）；在基层初诊的病人身上是否一样，目前不知道（Hermans 2013）。单看无力分不出肌腱病还是撕裂：空罐试验时患侧比健侧无力，可能提示肩袖病变，包括冈上肌腱病或撕裂（AAFP 2008）。

Imaging is not needed to confirm subacromial pain syndrome or to start treatment (BESS 2025). BESS lists plain radiographs for trauma, red flags, severe pain or stiffness, acromioclavicular joint arthritis, or subacromial pain lasting longer than four weeks. The Dutch update recommends ultrasound when a full- or partial-thickness supraspinatus tear is suspected, and MRI when ultrasound is unavailable or inconclusive (Lambers Heerspink 2026).

确诊肩峰下疼痛综合征和开始治疗都不需要影像（BESS 2025）。BESS 列出要拍 X 线平片的情况：外伤、有红旗征、疼痛或僵硬严重、肩锁关节炎，或肩峰下疼痛超过四周。荷兰指南更新版建议：怀疑冈上肌腱全层或部分撕裂时做超声，超声做不了或结果不明确时做 MRI（Lambers Heerspink 2026）。

Step 5: treatment in more detail第 5 步：治疗细讲

What the exercise looked like in a trial: Holmgren (2012) used eccentric strengthening of the rotator cuff plus concentric and eccentric exercises for the scapular stabilisers, combined with manual mobilisation. Patients had five or six supervised sessions over 12 weeks and did home exercises once or twice a day. Pain during the exercises was allowed, but it could not go above 5 on a 0–10 scale and had to settle back to the pre-exercise level before the next session; otherwise the load was reduced.

试验里的运动是什么样：Holmgren（2012）用肩袖离心强化，加上肩胛稳定肌的向心和离心训练，并配合手法松动。12 周内有 5–6 次治疗师指导，在家每天练 1–2 次。练习时允许疼痛，但不能超过 0–10 分的 5 分，而且下次练习前疼痛要回到练习前的水平；否则就减负荷。

Manual therapy: the Dutch guideline found that joint mobilisations add no benefit to an active exercise programme (Diercks 2014). A later meta-analysis found manual therapy plus exercise better than exercise alone only at the shortest follow-up, with a small effect (SMD −0.32, 95% CI −0.62 to −0.01), and rated all of its evidence very low quality (Steuri 2017).

手法治疗：荷兰指南认为，在主动运动之外加关节松动，没有额外好处（Diercks 2014）。后来的一项荟萃分析发现，手法加运动只在最短的随访时间点比单纯运动好，效应小（SMD −0.32，95% CI −0.62 到 −0.01），而且全部证据质量评为很低（Steuri 2017）。

Corticosteroid injection: in the first 8 weeks, subacromial injections reduce pain and improve function more than placebo injections, physiotherapy or no treatment, but the effect beyond 3 months is unclear, and an injection is not recommended as the only long-term treatment (Diercks 2014). The Dutch update suggests an injection to make exercise possible when severe pain stops the patient from taking part (Lambers Heerspink 2026); BESS considers one injection when there is no improvement by 6–8 weeks, as a shared decision with the patient (BESS 2025).

皮质类固醇注射：头 8 周内，肩峰下注射在减轻疼痛、改善功能上优于安慰注射、物理治疗或不治疗，但 3 个月以后的效果不明确，也不建议把注射当作唯一的长期治疗（Diercks 2014）。荷兰指南更新版建议：疼痛严重到没法参加运动治疗时，可以考虑注射，让运动做得下去（Lambers Heerspink 2026）；BESS 则是在 6–8 周仍无改善时，和病人共同决定是否打一针（BESS 2025）。

Acupuncture and dry needling: the 2014 Dutch guideline rated acupuncture as appearing no more effective than placebo or exercise therapy (level 3 evidence). It gives no recommendation on dry needling and says only that treatment of myofascial trigger points may be considered (Diercks 2014). Thursday's paper is a 2026 randomised trial of dry needling for subacromial pain, so needling gets its own day.

针刺与干针：2014 年荷兰指南认为，针刺看来不比安慰治疗或运动治疗更有效（3 级证据）。它对干针没有给推荐，只说可以考虑处理肌筋膜触发点（Diercks 2014）。周四要读的是一篇 2026 年干针治疗肩峰下疼痛的随机对照试验，针刺的问题那天专门讲。

Surgery: the Dutch guideline says subacromial pain should preferably be treated without surgery (Diercks 2014). Two placebo-controlled trials support this. In CSAW, both surgical groups did better than no treatment at 6 months, but the difference was not clinically important, and decompression added nothing over arthroscopy alone (Beard 2018). In FIMPACT, decompression gave no benefit over diagnostic arthroscopy at 24 months (Paavola 2018). A BMJ Rapid Recommendation panel then made a strong recommendation against the operation (Vandvik 2019). BESS (2025) still says surgery could be considered after six months of failed non-surgical treatment, so the sources disagree on its place late in the pathway; they agree it is not where treatment starts.

手术：荷兰指南说肩峰下疼痛最好不手术治疗（Diercks 2014）。两项有安慰对照的试验支持这一点。CSAW 试验中，两个手术组 6 个月时都比不治疗好，但差别没有临床意义，而且减压并不比单纯关节镜更好（Beard 2018）。FIMPACT 试验中，24 个月时减压术并不比诊断性关节镜更好（Paavola 2018）。随后 BMJ 快速推荐小组强烈建议不做这个手术（Vandvik 2019）。BESS（2025）仍然说，非手术治疗六个月无效后可以考虑手术。所以在流程后段手术放在哪里，来源之间有分歧；但都同意手术不是治疗的起点。

Step 6: progression in more detail第 6 步：进阶细讲

How much pain is acceptable during exercise is not settled. The Dutch guideline says stay within the pain threshold (Diercks 2014); Holmgren allowed up to 5 out of 10. Lewis (2016) suggests that in non-irritable cases, especially when progress has stalled, pain tolerated at about 2 to 5 out of 10 may be tried, as long as it settles soon after exercise and does not increase night pain or pain over 24 hours. Littlewood's self-managed programme lets exercise produce pain as long as symptoms are no worse once it stops, and progresses when that pain response settles (Littlewood 2013). A 2015 review found that producing or avoiding pain did not appear to change outcomes (Littlewood 2015), and BESS (2025) also calls the question unclear.

练习时能接受多痛，目前没有定论。荷兰指南说不超过疼痛阈值（Diercks 2014）；Holmgren 允许到 10 分里的 5 分。Lewis（2016）建议：不是易激惹型、尤其是进步停滞时，可以试着接受约 2–5 分、病人能耐受的疼痛，前提是练完很快缓解，而且不加重夜间痛和 24 小时内的疼痛。Littlewood 的自我管理方案允许练习时痛，只要停下后症状不比练之前重；疼痛反应消退了就进阶（Littlewood 2013）。2015 年的一篇综述发现，练习时痛或不痛，看来不影响结果（Littlewood 2015）；BESS（2025）也说这个问题不清楚。

For an irritable shoulder, Lewis (2016) notes that resistance work done quickly, under high loads and many repetitions, commonly aggravates symptoms. He suggests supported isometrics in the direction of pain to help control pain: some evidence from other regions supports this, but conclusive evidence for the shoulder is not available.

对易激惹型的肩，Lewis（2016）指出：速度快、负荷大、次数多的抗阻训练常常让症状加重。他建议在手臂有支撑的情况下，朝疼痛方向做等长收缩来控制疼痛；其他部位有一些证据支持，但肩部还没有确定的证据。

Time points from the guidelines: if symptoms last longer than 6 weeks, the Dutch guideline advises steps at work to prevent a chronic course and an ultrasound to rule out a cuff tear (Diercks 2014). BESS (2025) considers one injection at 6–8 weeks without improvement, refers to secondary care at 12 weeks and considers surgery at six months. A review of exercise trials says most programmes should show clinically significant results by 12 weeks (Littlewood 2015). No opened source gives numeric return-to-sport or return-to-work criteria such as strength ratios; the Dutch update advises building up exercise gradually within sport and work to increase how much load the cuff can bear (Lambers Heerspink 2026).

指南里的时间点：症状超过 6 周，荷兰指南建议在工作上采取措施，防止转成慢性，并做超声排除肩袖撕裂（Diercks 2014）。BESS（2025）是 6–8 周无改善考虑注射一针，12 周转专科，六个月考虑手术。一篇运动试验的综述说，多数方案到 12 周应该能看到有临床意义的效果（Littlewood 2015）。查到的来源都没有给出重返运动或工作的量化标准（比如肌力比值）；荷兰指南更新版建议在运动和工作中逐步加量，提高肩袖能承受的负荷（Lambers Heerspink 2026）。

易混点：“撞击”是一种解释，不是定论。图 01 这样的画法，容易让人以为是肩峰把肌腱压坏了。外源说认为肩峰的机械压迫造成肌腱损伤，内源说认为肌腱先退变；病因至今有争议，也就没有公认的首选疗法（Bifano 2014）。StatPearls 也写到，现在的文献不再把撞击看成单一的解剖诊断，而看成一组临床症状。两项有安慰对照的手术试验（CSAW、FIMPACT）中，减压术都没有比对照手术更好。向病人解释时，可以用荷兰指南更新版的说法：逐步加量的运动，是为了提高肩袖能承受的负荷（Lambers Heerspink 2026）。

What does the Dutch guideline's definition of subacromial pain syndrome describe, and what does BESS say should be absent?荷兰指南怎样定义肩峰下疼痛综合征？BESS 说哪些情况不应该存在？Show answer · 展开答案

Non-traumatic, usually one-sided pain around the acromion that often gets worse during or after lifting the arm. BESS: no trauma and no sign of a cuff tear, arthritis, frozen shoulder, instability, or neck or nerve problems.

非外伤性、通常单侧、位于肩峰周围、抬臂时或抬臂后常加重的肩痛。BESS：没有外伤，也没有肩袖撕裂、关节炎、冻结肩、不稳或颈椎与神经问题的表现。

Passive external rotation is as limited as active external rotation. Which two conditions come to mind, and which one needs X-rays to confirm it?被动外旋和主动外旋一样受限，要想到哪两种情况？哪一种必须拍 X 线确诊？Show answer · 展开答案

Frozen shoulder and glenohumeral osteoarthritis. Plain radiographs are essential to confirm osteoarthritis; in frozen shoulder the X-rays should be normal.

冻结肩和盂肱关节骨关节炎。骨关节炎确诊必须拍 X 线平片；冻结肩的 X 线应该正常。

Which two findings in this chapter need same-day referral?本章哪两种表现要当天转诊？Show answer · 展开答案

An acutely painful, red, warm joint (possible infection), and trauma followed by loss of rotation and an abnormal shape (unreduced dislocation).

关节急性疼痛、发红、发热（可能感染）；外伤后不能旋转、外形异常（未复位的脱位）。

In the Holmgren trial, what was the rule for pain during exercise?Holmgren 试验对练习时的疼痛怎么规定？Show answer · 展开答案

Pain could not go above 5 on a 0–10 scale, and it had to settle back to the pre-exercise level before the next session; otherwise the load was reduced.

不能超过 0–10 分的 5 分，而且下次练习前要回到练习前的水平；否则就减负荷。

Why is surgery not where treatment starts?为什么手术不是治疗的起点？Show answer · 展开答案

The Dutch guideline prefers non-operative treatment, and in two placebo-controlled trials (CSAW, FIMPACT) decompression was no better than arthroscopy alone or diagnostic arthroscopy.

荷兰指南首选非手术治疗；两项有安慰对照的试验（CSAW、FIMPACT）中，减压术并不比单纯关节镜或诊断性关节镜更好。

Take-home · 本章要点
Subacromial pain syndrome is a clinical diagnosis: history and examination first, imaging only for specific reasons, exercise at the core of treatment, and surgery not the place to start.肩峰下疼痛综合征是临床诊断：先问诊和查体，影像只在特定情况下做，运动是治疗核心，手术不是起点。
