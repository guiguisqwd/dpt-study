01

Anatomy: the four rotator cuff muscles解剖基础：肩袖的四块肌肉

After this chapter you can 读完本章，你能够 ·

  1. Name the four rotator cuff muscles in English and Chinese.用英文和中文说出肩袖的四块肌肉。
  2. State each muscle's origin and insertion and point to them on a scapula.说出每块肌肉的起点和止点，并能在肩胛骨上指出来。
  3. Explain why the cuff is mainly a stabilizer.解释为什么肩袖主要起稳定作用。

Key terms 关键词（点按钮听发音）

Supraspinatus冈上肌Infraspinatus冈下肌Teres minor小圆肌Subscapularis肩胛下肌Greater tubercle大结节Lesser tubercle小结节Glenoid cavity关节盂Acromion肩峰

The rotator cuff is formed by the supraspinatus, infraspinatus, teres minor, and subscapularis. All four arise from the scapula and attach to the proximal humerus, where their tendons blend into a cuff around the humeral head.

肩袖由冈上肌、冈下肌、小圆肌和肩胛下肌组成。四块肌肉都起自肩胛骨、止于肱骨近端，它们的肌腱在肱骨头周围汇合成一个“袖套”。

Three of the muscles lie behind the joint and attach to the greater tubercle; one lies in front and attaches to the lesser tubercle. Their main task is to keep the humeral head centred in the shallow glenoid cavity while larger muscles move the arm.

其中三块位于关节后方，止于大结节；一块位于前方，止于小结节。它们的主要任务是在大肌肉带动手臂时，把肱骨头稳定在较浅的关节盂中央。

Suprascapular nerve肩胛上神经Axillary nerve腋神经Subscapular nerves肩胛下神经Brachial plexus臂丛Superior trunk上干Posterior cord后束Spinoglenoid notch冈盂切迹Quadrangular space四边孔

C = Cervical · 颈部   T = Thoracic · 胸部

C5 vertebra第 5 颈椎：一块骨。
C5 spinal nerve第 5 颈神经：从 C4 与 C5 椎骨之间穿出。

All four cuff muscles receive fibres from the C5 and C6 spinal nerves. These fibres join in the superior trunk of the brachial plexus and then leave by two routes: the suprascapular nerve branches directly from the superior trunk, while the subscapular and axillary nerves arise later from the posterior cord.

肩袖四肌的神经纤维都来自 C5 和 C6 脊神经。这些纤维先汇入臂丛上干，再分两路离开：肩胛上神经直接从上干发出；肩胛下神经和腋神经则在更远处由后束发出。

Suprascapular nerve: anatomical course肩胛上神经：解剖走行

The suprascapular nerve runs laterally across the posterior triangle of the neck and reaches the superior border of the scapula. It passes through the suprascapular notch beneath the superior transverse scapular ligament, whereas the suprascapular artery usually passes above the ligament.

肩胛上神经向外侧横过颈后三角，到达肩胛骨上缘。神经经肩胛上切迹、从肩胛上横韧带下方穿过；肩胛上动脉通常从韧带上方经过。

In the supraspinous fossa it gives motor branches to the supraspinatus. It then turns around the lateral border of the scapular spine at the spinoglenoid notch and ends in the infraspinatus.

神经在冈上窝发出运动支支配冈上肌，随后在冈盂切迹绕过肩胛冈的外侧缘，最终进入冈下肌。

Compression at the suprascapular notch affects both supraspinatus and infraspinatus. Compression at the spinoglenoid notch affects only infraspinatus, because the supraspinatus branches have already left the nerve.

在肩胛上切迹受压时，冈上肌和冈下肌都受影响；在冈盂切迹受压时只影响冈下肌，因为支配冈上肌的分支已经先行发出。

记法：Army over Navy —— 动脉（Artery）走韧带上方，神经（Nerve）走韧带下方。

Axillary nerve: the quadrangular space腋神经：四边孔

The axillary nerve (C5–C6) arises from the posterior cord in the axilla. It passes posteriorly through the quadrangular space with the posterior circumflex humeral artery and divides into two branches.

腋神经（C5–C6）在腋窝由后束发出，与旋肱后动脉一起向后穿过四边孔，随后分为两支。

The posterior branch supplies the teres minor and continues as the superior lateral cutaneous nerve of the arm. The anterior branch winds around the surgical neck of the humerus and supplies the deltoid.

后支支配小圆肌，并延续为臂外上侧皮神经；前支绕肱骨外科颈走行，支配三角肌。

Subscapular nerves肩胛下神经

The upper and lower subscapular nerves (C5–C6) leave the posterior cord and enter the anterior surface of the subscapularis. The lower subscapular nerve also supplies the teres major.

上、下肩胛下神经（C5–C6）由后束发出，从前面进入肩胛下肌；下肩胛下神经还支配大圆肌。

Brachial plexus: from roots to peripheral nerves臂丛：从根到周围神经

The plexus is organized as roots, trunks, divisions, cords, and branches. The cuff pathway uses the superior trunk; the suprascapular nerve leaves it directly, and the other cuff nerves follow its posterior division into the posterior cord.

臂丛依次分为根、干、股、束、支。肩袖的通路经过上干：肩胛上神经直接从上干发出，其余肩袖神经沿上干的后股进入后束。

Three other nerves control the scapula: the accessory nerve (cranial nerve XI) supplies the trapezius, the dorsal scapular nerve (C5) supplies the rhomboids and levator scapulae, and the long thoracic nerve (C5–C7) supplies the serratus anterior.

另有三条神经控制肩胛骨：副神经（第 XI 对脑神经）支配斜方肌；肩胛背神经（C5）支配菱形肌和肩胛提肌；胸长神经（C5–C7）支配前锯肌。

Sources · 来源：StatPearls: Cervical nerves · StatPearls: Rotator Cuff

3.1 From attachment to action3.1 从起止点理解动作

The infraspinatus and teres minor pull the greater tubercle backward, behind the long axis of the humerus, so they rotate the humerus externally. The subscapularis pulls the lesser tubercle forward and medially, so it rotates the humerus internally.

冈下肌和小圆肌把大结节向后拉，力线经过肱骨长轴后方，因此使肱骨外旋；肩胛下肌把小结节向前内侧拉，因此使肱骨内旋。

The supraspinatus passes above the anteroposterior axis of the glenohumeral joint, so its pull raises the arm into abduction. The middle deltoid also passes above this axis and is the larger abductor.

冈上肌从盂肱关节前后轴的上方经过，因此它的拉力使手臂外展；三角肌中部同样经过这个轴的上方，是力量更大的外展肌。

3.2 Two force couples3.2 两组力偶

Acting alone, the deltoid would slide the humeral head upward toward the acromion. The supraspinatus compresses the head into the glenoid, and the infraspinatus, teres minor, and subscapularis pull it downward and medially. Together they turn the head in place.

三角肌单独收缩时，会把肱骨头向上推向肩峰。冈上肌把肱骨头压向关节盂，冈下肌、小圆肌和肩胛下肌把它向下、向内拉；合在一起，肱骨头在原位转动。

The upper trapezius pulls the lateral clavicle and acromion upward and medially; the lower trapezius pulls the root of the scapular spine downward and medially; the lower serratus anterior pulls the inferior angle laterally and forward. These three pulls rotate the glenoid cavity upward.

上斜方肌把锁骨外端和肩峰向上内拉；下斜方肌把肩胛冈根部向下内拉；前锯肌下部把肩胛下角向外、向前拉。三股拉力使关节盂朝上转动。

The axis of upward rotation lies near the root of the scapular spine early in elevation and moves toward the acromioclavicular joint later (Bagg & Forrest, 1988).

上旋的转动轴在上举早期靠近肩胛冈根部，后期移向肩锁关节（Bagg 与 Forrest，1988）。

3.3 Arm elevation and scapulohumeral rhythm3.3 手臂上举与肩肱节律

During the first 30° the scapula seeks a stable position (the setting phase), so most motion occurs at the glenohumeral joint. After that, glenohumeral and scapulothoracic motion occur in a ratio of about 2:1, giving roughly 120° and 60° at full elevation (Inman et al., 1944). Individual variation is large.

在前 30°，肩胛骨先寻找稳定位置（定位期），大部分活动发生在盂肱关节。此后盂肱关节与肩胛胸壁关节的活动比约为 2:1，上举到顶时约为 120° 与 60°（Inman 等，1944）。个体差异较大。

Initiation and setting起动与定位

Supraspinatus and deltoid start abduction.

冈上肌与三角肌一起起动外展。

The cuff holds the humeral head in the glenoid.

肩袖把肱骨头稳定在关节盂内。

Trapezius and serratus anterior stabilize the scapula.

斜方肌与前锯肌先把肩胛骨稳定住。

30–90°

Upward rotation begins上旋开始

GH : ST ≈ 2 : 1.

盂肱 : 肩胛胸壁约为 2 : 1。

Upper trapezius and serratus anterior start upward rotation.

上斜方肌与前锯肌带动上旋。

The clavicle elevates.

锁骨上抬。

90–180°

Rotation increases, humerus rotates externally上旋加大，肱骨外旋

Lower trapezius and serratus anterior dominate upward rotation.

下斜方肌与前锯肌成为上旋主力。

Infraspinatus and teres minor rotate the humerus externally so the greater tubercle clears the acromion.

冈下肌与小圆肌使肱骨外旋，让大结节从肩峰下方绕过。

The clavicle rotates posteriorly; the scapula tilts posteriorly.

锁骨向后旋转，肩胛骨后倾。

When the cuff is weak, the deltoid pulls the humeral head upward and narrows the subacromial space; pain then appears in the middle of the range, the painful arc of about 60–120°. Weak serratus anterior reduces upward rotation in the same way, and an overactive upper trapezius with a weak lower trapezius replaces rotation with shrugging.

肩袖无力时，三角肌把肱骨头向上拉，肩峰下间隙变窄，于是在活动范围中段（约 60–120°）出现疼痛弧。前锯肌无力同样会减少上旋；上斜方肌过度、下斜方肌无力时，会用耸肩代替上旋。

4.2 Nerve injury: sensory and motor findings4.2 神经损伤与感觉、运动表现

Which two cuff muscles share a nerve, and where can compression affect only one of them?哪两块肩袖肌由同一条神经支配？在哪里受压时只影响其中一块？Show answer · 展开答案

Why is the rotator cuff described as a stabilizer rather than a prime mover?为什么说肩袖主要是“稳定器”而不是“原动肌”？Show answer · 展开答案

Its muscles are small and lie close to the joint. Their main effect is to compress and centre the humeral head in the shallow glenoid while larger muscles such as the deltoid produce most of the motion.

肩袖的肌肉较小、紧贴关节。它们的主要作用是把肱骨头压紧并居中于较浅的关节盂，而三角肌等较大的肌肉产生大部分动作。

What are the borders of the quadrangular space, and what passes through it?四边孔的四条边是什么？哪些结构从中穿过？Show answer · 展开答案

It is bounded by teres minor above, teres major below, the long head of triceps medially, and the surgical neck of the humerus laterally. The axillary nerve and the posterior circumflex humeral artery pass through it.

上界为小圆肌，下界为大圆肌，内侧界为肱三头肌长头，外侧界为肱骨外科颈。腋神经和旋肱后动脉从中穿过。

Which muscles rotate the scapula upward, and which nerves supply them?哪些肌肉使肩胛骨上旋？分别由什么神经支配？Show answer · 展开答案

上斜方肌和下斜方肌（副神经支配）与前锯肌（胸长神经 C5–C7 支配）共同组成上旋力偶。

Why must the humerus rotate externally during full abduction?为什么完全外展时肱骨必须外旋？Show answer · 展开答案

From about 90° the greater tubercle approaches the acromion. External rotation, produced mainly by infraspinatus and teres minor, turns the tubercle backward so it can pass beneath the acromion.

约 90° 起，大结节接近肩峰。主要由冈下肌和小圆肌产生的外旋，把大结节转向后方，使它能从肩峰下方通过。

6.1 Research question and comparison groups6.1 研究问题与比较对象

In 121 participants with subacromial pain syndrome, the trial asked whether adding dry needling to manual therapy and exercise improves shoulder pain and disability more than the same physical therapy alone or with sham needling.

试验纳入 121 名肩峰下疼痛综合征患者，研究问题是：在手法治疗和运动治疗的基础上加用干针，是否比单纯物理治疗或加假干针更能改善肩痛与功能障碍。

6.4 Methodological terms6.4 方法学术语

  • Sham control · 假对照

    A placebo procedure that imitates the intervention, used to separate specific from non-specific effects.

    模仿干预过程的安慰操作，用来区分特异作用与非特异作用。

  • Primary vs secondary outcome · 主要结局与次要结局

    The primary outcome is fixed in advance and decides whether the trial is positive; secondary outcomes are supportive and exploratory.

    主要结局事先确定，决定试验结论是否阳性；次要结局只起支持和探索作用。

  • 95% confidence interval · 95% 置信区间

    The range of effects compatible with the data; an interval that crosses zero for a difference does not exclude no effect.

    与数据相容的效应范围；差值区间跨过 0，表示不能排除“没有效果”。

  • PASS · 患者可接受症状状态

    Patient Acceptable Symptom State: whether the patient considers the current state acceptable.

    Patient Acceptable Symptom State：患者认为当前症状是否可以接受。

  • Multiplicity · 多重比较

    Testing many outcomes and time points increases the chance that one appears significant by chance.

    检验的结局和时间点越多，偶然出现“显著”的机会越大。

6.5 Limits of this reading6.5 本次阅读的证据边界

Not verified from the abstract: needling sites, number of sessions, technique, adverse events, handling of missing data, correction for multiple comparisons, and the reference group for the odds ratio. These must be checked in the full text before any clinical conclusion.

摘要中无法核实的内容：针刺部位、次数、手法、不良事件、缺失数据处理、多重比较校正，以及 OR 的参照组。得出任何临床结论之前，都需要核对全文。

6.6 Conclusion within its boundary6.6 在边界内的结论

By its prespecified primary outcome this is a negative trial: adding dry needling did not significantly improve SPADI at 1 year. The PASS and care-seeking results favour dry needling, but as secondary outcomes they are signals for further study, not proof of benefit.

按事先设定的主要结局，这是一项阴性试验：加用干针在 1 年时没有显著改善 SPADI。PASS 和再就医结果偏向干针组，但它们属于次要结局，只能作为进一步研究的线索，不能证明有效。

For a reader trained in acupuncture: dry needling is reasoned from myofascial trigger points and neuroanatomy, whereas acupuncture is reasoned from meridians and acupoints. Common trigger regions of the posterior cuff lie close to Bingfeng, Tianzong, and Jianzhen, but this abstract does not report which sites were needled, so no acupoint can be attributed to this trial.

从针灸背景来看：干针依据肌筋膜触发点和神经解剖，针灸依据经络与腧穴。肩袖后部常见的触发点区域靠近秉风、天宗、肩贞，但本摘要没有报告针刺部位，因此不能把任何穴位归到这项试验上。
